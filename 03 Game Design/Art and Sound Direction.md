## Place identity

**Rocca Selva:** irregular limestone steps, worn communal thresholds, red thread on bells, lichened roofs, chestnut drying rooms, pasture tracks. Most homes were adapted rather than replaced when miracles arrived. A small delight: a grandmother hangs laundry on a line that climbs a gentle thermal creature's resting current.

**Istravel:** bright public surfaces, vertical gardens, bridges with woven living supports, civic art showing engineers and keepers as heroes. Avoid making every surface white and sterile. Repairs, markets, leisure, class distinctions, and old neighborhoods make the city inhabited.

**Orthe:** layered stone, water clocks, shaded assembly courts, hand-maintained gates, carefully delimited gardens. Restraint is an aesthetic of stewardship and public legibility, not universal gray austerity. Excluded people and unofficial experiments complicate the image.

**The Confluence:** several incompatible aesthetics. One community grows a common ceremonial canopy; another uses precise wearable supports; another celebrates visibly different bodies rather than a unified shape. Do not make altered bodies visual shorthand for evil.

These are visual briefs. [World Overview](../01%20World/World%20Overview.md) and individual location pages own setting facts.

## Shared treatment in the scene

These working review rules respond to the Glassgrazer mismatch; they are an assistant proposal tested by the v2 study (working-archive reference), not an approved final style.

- Review one person, the creature and their surroundings together at ordinary play distance and close interaction distance, in the same daylight and blue-hour conditions. An isolated illustration can succeed while its game appearance fails.
- Prefer readable broad material and light areas before adding small surface detail. Compare edge sharpness, apparent pixel size and detail density across actors and scenery; extra folds and highlights must contribute at the camera's real distance.
- Coordinate colours, light direction, shadow depth and contact while preserving each material's identity. Muting or simplifying a mineral fan must not accidentally make it look like fabric or a leaf.
- Distinct anatomy and personality do not require an unrelated rendering treatment. Preserve ambitious creature designs while making them inhabit the same visual world as the cast.

The current v2 matching density is a local experiment. Future camera, resolution and material choices may change it; neither eight headings nor one pixel density is a permanent requirement.

## Creature design grammar

Begin with body, movement, niche, and personality. Select one strong impossibility rather than piling on unrelated symbols. Preserve a readable silhouette, recognizable resting pose, and an expressive behavior. A creature should be interesting when it eats, waits, plays, or refuses, not only when attacking.

For each species, explore a small range of silhouettes, choose an anatomical plan, test motion, and define what must stay invariant across concept images and final assets. Model prompts are production recipes, not canonical descriptions of anatomy.

Dangerous species need not all be angular and black. A soft, beautiful animal can be a predator; a grotesque scavenger can be shy and ecologically valuable. Ordinary charm should sometimes remain ordinary charm.

## Horror through relationships between familiar elements

Early: the wrong echo in an otherwise lovely courtyard; a communal song with a missing call-and-response; a reflection that seems plausible until the player changes position.

Middle: two rooms share one soundscape despite lying far apart; public statues develop the same habitual gesture as residents; a companion greets an empty chair as though a familiar person occupies it.

Late: spaces fulfill an anticipated destination before the player chooses it; a civic celebration becomes synchronized beyond any rehearsal; a distant shape makes several buildings read as parts of one moving body.

These effects require authored context and reliable triggers. They are not approved events until linked to the narrative's evidence and reveal plan.

## Recognition before rupture

The transcript's recurring figures and human/creature correspondences suggest a useful visual discipline: establish identity through more than a label, then preserve selected details through change. These are original design proposals, not descriptions of shots we have viewed.

Give an important person or creature a small **recognition set**: one bodily habit, one distinctive sound, and one material detail. Repeat it in ordinary situations without framing every repetition as ominous. A keeper smooths a blanket twice before leaving it; a companion settles to a three-note whistle with an awkward pause; a handmade clasp has a visibly crooked repair. Any one could be coincidence. Together they can support a later inference.

When an unfamiliar structure repeats the gesture, do not immediately display a label announcing its identity. Let the player change position, compare an independent object, or ask the affected person to respond. Give a skeptical explanation a fair test. Familiarity should supply evidence, not make the whole cast secretly interchangeable.

## Three proposed visual beats

| Beat | Composition and sensory emphasis | What the player can establish |
|---|---|---|
| **A welcome that has learned too much** | First establish a sunny refuge entrance used by companions. Later, the same arrangement of bedding, water, and return markers appears at human scale, with an exit facing inward | The same care logic has been extended to people; this alone does not prove whether they chose it |
| **The absent worker's hand** | An ordinary maintenance practice repeats in unfamiliar moving architecture: two smoothing motions, then a pause at the same damaged clasp. Keep the material beautiful and physically specific before revealing its unsettling scale | Several independent identity cues correspond; a label or uncanny resemblance alone is insufficient |
| **The look beyond the traveler** | A familiar companion makes its ordinary request toward the player viewpoint while the protagonist is visibly elsewhere. Retain its normal call, lighting, and surroundings; avoid an obligatory glitch splash | A late scene distinguishes avatar from observer; the eventual camera design must make that distinction legible |

These beats are alternatives or scene components, not three mandatory reveals. The current [quest index](../02%20Narrative/Quest%20Index.md) and [horror design](../02%20Narrative/Horror%20and%20Fourth%20Wall.md) own actual placements. A finalized shot sequence must follow the selected camera and scene anatomy rather than overrule them.

## Body horror with an identifiable loss

A hand-shaped fold of living architecture can be more disturbing when it retains a worker's tired gesture than when it is merely covered in wounds. If physical transformation is chosen, define what the changed person can still sense, express, remember, refuse, and do. Let a response interrupt the player's first interpretation. The creature or changed body must have its own behavior and experience.

Grotesque detail can be used later where it contributes to the scene, but the initial world does not need graphic fragments to advertise its future darkness. Deliberate transhuman lives and ordinary unfamiliar anatomy should retain their own beauty; a coerced change is disturbing because of the circumstances and lost agency, not because all changed bodies are corrupt.

## Visual review questions for later

Can the recurring cue be noticed in normal play without a forced close-up? Does the altered form remain recognizable without copied costume colors or an explanatory nameplate? Does the clue survive reduced detail and small-screen viewing? Can the player check the claim through action? Does the reveal provide new understanding rather than just a stronger image? Actual footage and later prototypes would be needed to judge execution.

## Sound

**September desired experience:** the user asks for charming, mellifluous music, eccentric characters and contrasting regional moods. [Everyday Joy, Trade and Appearance](Everyday%20Joy%20Trade%20and%20Appearance.md) develops lyrical motifs, performances and melodies that retain sincere comfort; [Atmospheric Frontiers](../01%20World/Atmospheric%20Frontiers.md) supplies mist/canopy/snow/old-quay atmosphere briefs. This is wiki-only direction, not an audio commission or acceptance of one soundtrack style. Additional Italian bell work remains deferred and existing ambience unchanged.

Additional Italian bell work is deferred by the latest user clarification (D-032); existing ambience is unchanged. The broader sound ideas below remain draft direction rather than a request to generate or replace audio now.

Give places acoustic routines: bells, work songs, water, migrating calls, market rhythms. Give companions recognizable nonverbal sounds. Establish these patterns long enough for alteration to mean something.

Silence should be a specific absence. If a mill normally answers the evening bell, its failure to do so is more meaningful than a generic drone. Some apparently sinister sounds should have harmless causes so that the world retains texture.
