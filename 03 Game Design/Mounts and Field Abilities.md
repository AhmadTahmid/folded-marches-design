# Mounts and field abilities

The user wants several useful mounts across land, air, and water, and reasons to value monsters beyond battle. Five creatures fight together in the selected combat-party direction. Field arrangements below are proposed separately; a mount need not replace a favorite fighter, consume a combat move slot, or make an extra combat action available.

## A travel family, not a compulsory species

The six new [Bestiary](../01%20World/Bestiary.md) species establish contrasting mount experiences. Shared ability families allow later species to offer different personalities and route choices without creating a new key for every locked path.

| Family and initial example | Pleasant mastery | Boundary | Route alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open-ground stride — Brackenstride | Preserve momentum, judge broad clear landings, find a ridge shortcut | Loose scree, blind gaps, dense canopy | Footpath or caravan road |
| Stone grip — Cragmantle | Read surfaces, climb steadily, discover small resting shelves | No ceilings, crumbling rock, or arbitrary wall climbing | Roped traverse, maintained stair, local climbing guide |
| Thermal soar — Sailrook | Link rising columns and choose landing shelves | Needs lift and launch room; no hovering or storm flight | High trail or a later established route service |
| Sheltered hover — Vesperveil | Approach awkward ledges quietly and hold a precise position | Short flights in cool still air; no fast open-country travel | Climbing line, branch route, nearby lift where authored |
| Surface passage — Reedbarque | Follow channels, stop beside wildlife, carry a small expedition | Slow in strong flow; no rider dives or salt-pan travel | Boat, ferry, bank path |
| Current passage — Gorgecoil | Read flow lanes and cross between known surfacing pockets | Requires connected suitable water; limited rider air | Dry gallery, bridge, engineered lift |

First journeys reveal the constraints before asking for a commitment. A launch preview shows usable currents and safe landings; a dive preview shows the next surfacing pocket and return. Weather closes an unsafe approach with clear cues. It should create a new plan, not ambush an airborne player with unavoidable failure.

No mount supplies unrestricted world flight or turns every visible surface into playable terrain. Geography still matters because skills address different parts of a landscape. A place reached from above should acknowledge that arrival; a plot door must not become the only functional exit from an otherwise climbable ruin.

## Partners beyond riding

| Family | Existing candidate | Reward for understanding the animal |
|---|---|---|
| Scent and soil reading | Thimbleback | Follow a displaced seed cache to a changed drain or buried terrace edge |
| Heat care | Embermole | Make a short cold rest comfortable when a suitable heat source exists; an ordinary hearth remains sufficient |
| Path calls and signals | Bellwether | Locate a herd through fog and compare echoes along a route, with visual/text equivalents |
| Light courier work | Kitefin | Carry a marker or line over a small obstacle; it cannot carry the protagonist |
| Current reading | Ribbonwake | Locate a stable crossing or show why an upstream intervention changed a pool |
| Nest and seam recognition | Doorling | Indicate a usable local opening or a changed building edge; never unlock every door |
| Mineral interpretation | Glassgrazer | Reveal relevant deposits through behavior, with instruments and local expertise as other sources |

These are candidate uses consistent with established ecologies, not automatic new supernatural powers. A clue remains observable without a particular partner; the companion can point it out earlier, add detail, or reveal a different solution. Working with a wild population may create a route that no individual companion can reproduce.

## Field access without party chores

Proposal: the expedition journal lists bonded travel partners and available local partnerships separately from the five-member combat team. At a safe approach, select an eligible partner from a compatible portable refuge or established nearby shelter/service. Exact remote availability and refuge sizes remain open; the interface must state which travel partners are available before departure. Suitable local rentals or introductions support major routes before the player owns a specialist.

Only the current travel partner needs to accompany the rider visibly. Changing it at a safe approach should be brief. A field-only partner retreats before a battle and contributes no extra fighter, passive combat bonus, or hidden sixth action. If that same individual is one of the five combat members, it participates normally. Acquired field practices do not replace learned combat skills.

No routine hunger timer, repeated trust maintenance, single-use traversal fuel, or compulsory crafted lure belongs in this proposal. Basic travel arrangements cover ordinary care. Distinctive needs appear in meaningful route choices and scenes, not a recurring menu tax. Cooking, crafting, and base building remain deferred options; these systems require none of them.

## Ownership is only one form of access

A catch can make a favorite method familiar and expressive: a mount greets the same ferry worker, favors a particular rest ledge, and develops practiced signals with its rider. Local partnerships let a player try a method before recruiting. Public infrastructure provides a third path. Optional mastery routes can strongly favor a capability, while main routes remain accessible through more than one species or non-creature solution.

Knuckle Saints remain uncatchable and are not converted into giant taxis. Morrow Moths remain wild. Keeping some encounters outside ownership makes a partnership feel specific rather than a claim that every living thing exists to serve the player.
