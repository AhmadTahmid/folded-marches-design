# Game direction

**v0.1 reading:** [Adventure Rules v0.1](Adventure%20Rules%20v0.1.md) selects one working interpretation for this edition. The comparisons below remain useful alternatives; they are not all simultaneous requirements. User-selected turn-based combat and the five-active ceiling still govern.

Design proposals for discussion. This document describes intended whole-game experiences; a separate small browser benchmark implements some draft elements but does not validate these complete systems. Current work is comprehensive wiki development only (D-047).

## The experience in a sentence

A creature-companion adventure in which learning how a living place works gives you new ways to travel, help people, and recognize when a beautifully functioning world has begun to erase the possibility of saying no.

## What a good ordinary hour could contain

Leave a village market with a picnic, a borrowed field instrument, and a lead on an elusive animal. Follow its feeding signs above a river. Help a shepherd reroute a wandering flock around a singing sinkhole. Discover a shortcut your companion enjoys but cannot yet cross with you. Meet a courier taking an impossible bridge to the city. Resolve a creature encounter through observation, negotiation, or battle. Return with a new friendship, a changed route, and a question that makes tomorrow's journey attractive.

Only occasionally should the last discovery be frightening. The game must sustain curiosity, satisfying encounters, and companionship between major revelations.

## Working pillars

| Pillar | What the player actually does | Design check |
|---|---|---|
| A world worth living in | Enjoys food, festivals, useful miracles, journeys, personalities | Can this area sustain a pleasant visit without its secret? |
| Creatures with their own lives | Observes routines, learns preferences, offers partnership | Does this species have needs unrelated to the player? |
| Power with dependencies | Chooses who maintains a route, clinic, or accord | Can benefits and costs be traced to particular people? |
| Freedom with consequences | Reaches places in several orders and revisits changed communities | Does the story acknowledge knowledge already acquired? |
| Earned unease | Notices repeatable contradictions and tests explanations | Could a careful player distinguish a clue from a continuity mistake? |

## Play loops

**Moment to moment:** read terrain → move with companions → notice behavior → act → receive clear feedback.

**Expedition:** choose an intention → prepare a small team → explore connected routes → manage fatigue and opportunities → form relationships or solve a local problem → return or establish a resting point.

**Regional:** gain access and trust → understand a community's dependency → decide which intervention to support → see a tangible change → discover a wider consequence.

**Long term:** improve the material prospects of his mother and three younger siblings, pursue a life he wants beyond the mountain, develop a team and lasting relationships, and face new responsibilities alongside those he already carries. Connecting rival societies and investigating the Largesse can follow those stakes; neither replaces the protagonist's personal reason to continue. See [Personal Story](../02%20Narrative/Personal%20Story.md).

## Working mechanical direction

- The user selected a maximum of five friendly combat creatures, all active together, with positioning and coordinated abilities. Early parties may contain fewer; additional partners rest outside the battle.
- Strategic, technical turn-based combat is selected, with Dota 2 as the main mechanical inspiration. The rules in [Tactical Combat](Tactical%20Combat.md) and [Status and Counterplay](Status%20and%20Counterplay.md) translate counterplay and timing into proposed turns; the exact model and values remain untested.
- Visible encounters where feasible, discoverable rare opportunities, and condition-based searches such as rain and night. [Rare Encounters](Rare%20Encounters.md) separates delightful chance from reliable progress through investigation.
- Multiple land, air, and water mounts and other field roles reward discovery. Specific species and access rules are proposed in [Mounts and Field Abilities](Mounts%20and%20Field%20Abilities.md).
- Exploration skills broaden routes rather than binding every route to one mandatory species.
- Relationships and institutional commitments matter alongside individual creature growth.
- An open region with several interlinked journeys, rather than a promise of a seamless continent at this stage.
- Levels remain meaningful, while catch-up, shared experience, and candidate talent forks reduce repetition. [Progression and Talents](Progression%20and%20Talents.md) describes the proposal.
- Cooking, crafting, and base building are deferred options. Current combat and exploration loops must function without them.

See [Companionship and Catching](Companionship%20and%20Catching.md), [Encounter Design](Encounter%20Design.md), and [Exploration and Progression](Exploration%20and%20Progression.md).

[Creature Activities](Creature%20Activities.md) develops three optional pastimes with concrete invitations, choices, rewards, and return visits. They provide ordinary fun without requiring a political or horror interpretation.

## Decisions deliberately left open

The September design expansion (working-archive reference) adds desired clutch intensity, personal companion quests, fewer filler battles and environmental engagement. [Tension, Stakes and Recovery](Tension%20Stakes%20and%20Recovery.md) compares mastery, attachment and reaction pressure while keeping selected turn-based combat. [Recruitment, Eggs and Binding Instruments](Recruitment%20Eggs%20and%20Binding%20Instruments.md), [Resonance, Fusion and Last Resorts](Resonance%20Fusion%20and%20Last%20Resorts.md), [Travel, Mapping and Pursuit](Travel%20Mapping%20and%20Pursuit.md) and [Everyday Joy, Trade and Appearance](Everyday%20Joy%20Trade%20and%20Appearance.md) develop alternatives rather than adopt all their systems. The user's latest consequence answer asks for recovery/rescue comparisons; no permadeath baseline is selected.

Exact combat timing and geometry; technique counts and balance; talent structure and level curve; field-roster access; evolution or other transformations; protagonist customization and remaining biographical details within the selected age/family premise; late-game tone; world scale; exact camera; how far fourth-wall effects extend; whether collection completion is a meaningful goal. Turn-based mode, the five-active maximum, and the personal departure motive are now selected; the remaining details stay reviewable.

## Promise versus evidence

A compelling wiki can establish intent and expose contradictions. It cannot establish that a combat loop is fun, the entire map runs on mobile, or a particular art pipeline produces enough content. Those claims need later prototypes. [Scope and Validation](Scope%20and%20Validation.md) records the questions without starting implementation.
