# Resonance, fusion and last resorts

**v0.1 reading:** [Adventure Rules v0.1](Adventure%20Rules%20v0.1.md) selects one working interpretation for this edition. The comparisons below remain useful alternatives; they are not all simultaneous requirements. User-selected turn-based combat and the five-active ceiling still govern.

The user proposes an innate that helps collection, a Cassette Beasts-like fusion hook tied to story, and dramatic trump cards such as a revealed defense, arriving allies or a second form. These are useful questions, not selected systems. The turn-based direction and five-active ceiling remain fixed; the father and protagonist identity options remain open.

External evidence is in Adrenaline and Creature Design Research (working-archive reference). Its official-source descriptions establish reference features, not this game's rules or every fusion pair's feasibility. The Naruto/Souls examples are the user's analogy for dramatic reversal; no scene, power or asset is copied.

## Resonance as affinity: compare three causes

The existing combat rules use **Resonance** as a damage category. **Affinity** below refers to a proposed relationship/perception capability, not that damage stat. Do not let the shared word decide numerical bonuses or cosmology.

| Version | How it helps collection | Narrative fit | Risk and proposed limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Learned attention | Read calls, posture and environmental patterns more clearly through practice | Fits an ordinary young worker whose competence expands | Never requires grinding the same animal; learning produces new information, not ownership |
| Widespread innate variation | Some people notice particular accord patterns more readily; training still matters | Allows a personal tendency without exclusive destiny | The protagonist is not uniquely compatible with everything; other keepers show strengths he lacks |
| Acquired interface | An expedition grants access to a new perceptual channel, instrument or relationship | Connects collecting and an observer plot through a specific event | Must not become an unexplained divine gift, father reveal or compulsory loss of self |

**Editorial recommendation:** begin with learned attention or ordinary innate variation. Let a later specific channel broaden perception if it earns a scene. All versions can make existing conditions legible rather than increase a hidden capture percentage. Compatibility can reveal whom to approach, how to help and what a creature refuses. It cannot manufacture consent.

Collection incentives include discovering another field role, a contrasting tactical answer, a personal interaction or a relationship technique. Avoid secretly requiring complete ownership of a roster to unlock the final truth. Species observation and wild partnerships can matter too.

## Three fusion hooks to compare

| Hook | What the player sees and does | Why it might be fun | Story implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Concord technique** | Two distinct creatures perform a combined action with a memorable shared gesture | Team discovery and spectacular moves without losing cherished designs | Cooperation retains two participants who can stop independently |
| **Temporary shared form** | Two creatures become one visibly new body for a stated encounter window | Transformation, a different silhouette and a powerful tactical change | A merger can be voluntary and reversible; coercive examples violate those specific boundaries |
| **Borrowed perception** | The traveler briefly experiences a companion's senses or field movement | Environmental discovery and embodied connection | Distinguishes sharing a perspective from taking over a living being |

These are alternatives for the central hook, not three compulsory systems. The third may be limited to scenes or observation if direct creature control would turn the project into a second action game. The attachment's “one power expressed three ways” is a possible unifying theory, not authority to combine everything.

## Candidate battle rules that preserve the ceiling

For a concord technique, reserve both participants' unused activations. Confirmation spends both; the move does not grant a bonus sixth action or trigger recursive reactions. Preparation is revisable until confirmed. If one creature is unavailable or legally prevented from participating, show why and leave the uncommitted pair's choices available. Exact costs, target tags and cooldowns require a later numerical design.

In this candidate, the combined sequence resolves at the current friendly decision, then the enemy receives its usual next listed activation. Both contributors remain marked as already activated; neither can be selected again that round. Consuming a second friendly activation does not silently grant or remove an enemy activation. The remaining round proceeds under the existing queue rule. This explicit timing is a proposed exception, not a change to the adopted baseline.

For a shared form, two existing friendly bodies become **one**; the team never exceeds five. Both component activations count as spent when combining. The form gains no fresh activation that round; in a later round it receives one normal activation. On separation, components return to their prior legal slots if possible, or disclosed empty slots; neither gains a new activation in that round. Two partners' status effects, HP mapping, protection and exhaustion need an explicit contract before this model can be balanced. Do not borrow the reference game's rules by assumption.

A form can trade two independent answers for one powerful capability. That gives it a real cost beyond a meter. An effect that produces spectacular damage while making every normal strategy worse would become a required animation rather than a choice.

## Discovery without promising every combination

Start on paper with two memorable pairs and one incompatible or unproductive pair. A discovery has clues: animals rehearse compatible rhythms, habitat roles complement each other, or a keeper demonstrates the principle. The player can experiment with a preview that states participation and cost; failures do not consume a beloved individual.

**Example concord, not a finished technique:** one partner gathers resonance while another shapes a visible shelter. Their coupled rhythm protects a crossing, then ends with both still present. Their ordinary gestures remain recognizable. This is not an automatic assignment of a new power to Bellwether or Thimbleback; species-compatible pairings need their kit pages reviewed.

An ambitious pair space grows rapidly: an unordered collection of N species has N(N−1)/2 distinct two-species pairs before same-species and individual variants. Custom artwork for every pair is not promised. Compare selected authored pair forms, compatible families and cooperative techniques preserving individual bodies. Production cost does not define fictional possibility, but the wiki must state whether a combination is a full body, action or story-only relation.

## Trump cards that reveal a person or creature

A surprising reversal is valuable when it changes the tactical question and says something about its user. It is weaker when every opponent simply restores HP without warning. Foreshadow the capability, preserve a chance to respond and avoid requiring the player to replay a long unchanged first phase.

| Last resort | Proposal | Counterplay and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Protective manifestation | An established keeper commits a finite apparatus or a creature's practiced shelter to defend a specific person | The protection has a visible duration, upkeep or anchor; attack another objective or break the shown source |
| Call for help | An existing active creature spends its activation to signal an ally, which enters an empty active slot or replaces an explicitly withdrawn combatant | No sixth friendly body; no free activation for the arriving unit that round. Reserve-entry rules remain an encounter exception until separately adopted |
| Second form | A wounded entity sheds an outer structure or a voluntary merged form separates | It stays one actor unless a disclosed split rule changes roster occupancy; the next intent is readable and earlier knowledge still helps |
| One revival | A prepared technique restores a defeated partner through a stated resource | Counts against that round's five activations; no revival chain, surprise full reset or universal doctrine about souls |
| Companion refusal | A trusted creature recognizes that an expected gesture completes containment and warns | Narrative exception is previewed before final confirmation; offer a valid alternative. Not a random disobedience percentage |

This extends the paper design, not the current prototype. [Tactical Combat](Tactical%20Combat.md) presently excludes ordinary mid-battle reserve replacement; a call-for-help encounter must be labelled an exception or remain a draft until that owning rule changes.

## An enemy reversal to write before balancing

A rival has repeatedly practiced a defensive preparation that seems overly cautious. During a later objective fight, they expend the apparatus to cover retreating workers, leaving their strongest creature unable to pursue. The visual reveal is impressive; the cost demonstrates what they value. The player can challenge the apparatus, hold the alternative route or accept an incomplete objective.

For an eldritch second form, establish cracks, a different shadow or an earlier witnessed discarded shell. The transformation changes reach, rhythm or objective—not every mechanic at once. Preserve accumulated tactical progress and provide a checkpoint or shortened re-entry if the encounter fails. One well-prepared second form is more memorable than routine resurrection on every boss.

## Tie cooperation to the horror without condemning it

A healthy merger keeps participants recognizable, supports a stated withdrawal and gives each a channel of expression. A coercive apparatus eliminates one of those conditions. The distinction can be tested through an agreed signal or a bounded separation attempt. Do not imply that altered bodies are inherently evil or that every fusion user has lost their humanity.

The observer might imitate a concord signal to obtain access, while a companion notices the timing is wrong. That connects a loved mechanic to a specific threat. Ordinary cooperation remains effective elsewhere. Exact source, innate, pair system, story phase and final transformation rules remain open.
