# Battles during the opening day

The opening offers a first expressive move, a cooperative objective, a rival's answer and one optional wild confrontation. It does not require all four, and none is a gate to Pell's wage or onward travel. [Tactical Combat](Tactical%20Combat.md) and [First Region Creature Kits](First%20Region%20Creature%20Kits.md) remain the numerical owners. All creatures and commands stay visible; no hidden alteration of learned moves forces a tutorial solution.

## F5-B01 — the crooked pennant, Pass House court

Use the complete **learning encounter 1** in [Combat Learning Sequence](Combat%20Learning%20Sequence.md#1-the-crooked-pennant--choose-what-one-partner-does): friendly benchmark Bellwether, opposed by Mara's explicitly declared 18-HP practice Bellwether. Both front; player opens round 1. Strike and Peal lead to different legal outcomes. Free loan if needed; accepting a different partner requires its declared pairing rather than pretending every species has Peal. The supplied Bellwether loan always makes the authored fixture available.

**Location:** the first-five-hours version uses RS-08b, the Pass House loading/practice court. An invitation after Clatter's walk and a sign from Cistern Crown via Rope Walk both lead there. A battle never blocks drinking water or breeding shade. The representative route offers it in minutes 27–45; this is a pacing estimate, not a timer. No completed survey or side story is needed.

Win exhausts the opponent; lose exhausts the friendly; surrender ends immediately. Restore both partners after any result. A loan is returned, no XP or item can be farmed, and the player's own partner remains theirs. Reward is its characteristic response and the player's experience, not survey eligibility.

## F5-B02 — the flag under the washing line, exchange practice rail

Use **learning encounter 2**, with one front friendly, one rear friendly and the benchmark defending Thimbleback. Disclosed loans allow participation without collecting two species. A tiny practice pennant has a separate marker from any public work cargo.

The flag remains covered until the defender has spent/lost its activation that round, or is exhausted. A standing ally in Rear 1 then spends its action to retrieve it and win. This makes the front action relevant. Guard, interruption, attacking while accepting damage, or exhausting the defender are alternatives. The objective and its availability appear before the player confirms entry. No new technique, capture or main-quest reward follows.

## F5-B03 — Rovan's second answer, exchange keeper circle

Use **learning encounter 3**, with Rovan's front Bellwether and rear Glassgrazer, and the declared friendly Bellwether/Thimbleback comparison or player-owned alternatives. Rovan's glass-fan rescuer acts first in the enemy queue, removing a blocked partner's Stagger before the charge. The shown Clod Flick answer demonstrates that Resolve blocks Stagger, not every restriction.

The invitation is named **Try Rovan's two-partner challenge**; it must not launch an unspecified generic fight. Full authored cards and enemy fallback rules come from that learning encounter. Surrender, loss and victory restore supervised participants. No entry fee, mandatory rare ally or story lock. His post-battle line reflects only the actual result: win **“Well played. I'll need a better plan.”**; loss **“Want another try? Watch which of mine acts first.”**; surrender **“Another time, then.”** He neither invents a tactic the player used nor treats defeat as unfitness for paid work.

## F5-B04 / RS-C10 — the owner of the short path

**Where:** a short lookout spur beside BR-A3 Strap Shelter. The main survey route and both marker access paths remain clear. The reward beyond it is a view through paired chestnuts toward the passing caravans, not a hidden required report point. A wild Thimbleback has collected bright discarded washers under a loose stone at the spur's edge. It mistakes the swinging metal parts on a travel bag for an intrusion toward its cache.

First the player sees disturbed husks, then a thrown clod landing deliberately short, then the animal fold over its cups. Captions and the inspection rail communicate the warning without sound. There is no surprise battle trigger on merely walking close. **Choose:** return to the public road; wait at the observation rail; show an ordinary unclaimed pebble and give the cache space; or explicitly send a companion to stand its ground. Fighting requires a standing owned travel companion; the other approaches do not. Practice loans are not quietly taken into the wilderness.

The noncombat approach uses a pebble already visible on the rail, not a purchased gift or a hidden favorite-color test. Put it on the near flat stone and step to the wide edge. The animal stores it, then returns to its cups while the player passes at the indicated distance. Waiting observes the same safe distance when it turns to sort; there is no real-time reaction test. The player can still choose to leave. No approach opens a recruitment invitation: this is an established individual at its cache, distinct from RS-C02.

**Declared wild fixture:** one Thimbleback, Front 1, **18 HP**, Armor 2, Ward 0, Focus 3. This is a local encounter value, not a rewrite of the benchmark's 30 HP. It uses the existing Nut Pitch and Cupguard cards: odd rounds Nut Pitch at the lowest-numbered standing friendly front slot, otherwise first rear; even rounds Cupguard. Nut Pitch is 8 Impact, Focus 1/cooldown 1; Cupguard grants self Barrier 12, Focus 1/cooldown 2. An Attack prevented by Disarm becomes Guard; otherwise apply displayed legal targeting/fallback rules. It retains common Strike/Guard/Move and the baseline statuses. Player opens round 1; opening side alternates normally.

Use the player's present roster and current HP with the usual fresh-battle Focus, up to five active as always. Expected first visit is one or two; collecting more makes it easier rather than triggering an invisible level adjustment. Melee partners need front slots; the preparation screen explains this before committing. Do not silently put a lone melee creature in the rear.

**Victory:** opposing HP reaches zero; the exhausted animal withdraws behind its cache and recovers out of reach. It is neither killed nor collected. The lookout route becomes available for this visit. **Withdrawal:** any friendly activation can use this scene's Physical, non-Attack Withdraw for 0 Focus, ending at the observation rail. Stagger must be consumed or cleansed before that creature can act; another eligible ally can withdraw instead. **Defeat:** all friendly creatures exhausted; recover the party at Strap Shelter with observations and inventory intact, then take the public road or retry. Show these terms before the challenge.

**Small legal example:** friendly Bellwether and Embermole begin with their benchmark bodies, Bellwether in front and Embermole rear. Bellwether's Strike hits for 8 − 2 Armor = 6, leaving the wild animal at 12. Nut Pitch then hits Bellwether for 8. Embermole's Furnace Breath costs 1 Focus and deals 12 Resonance against Ward 0, finishing the confrontation. The plan shares damage and uses a second partner. Peal first would prevent that hit but leaves less damage this round; a lone Bellwether must respect the alternating shield windows. These are readable choices, not a required best answer.

The route reward is the view and a field-guide observation of this individual's behavior. No repeated money, rare loot or XP grind is introduced. A later arrival can see the same individual sorting washers; permanent death, automatic taming and endless cloned defenders are excluded. The sighting is not a sign of cosmic corruption. Ordinary wildlife can create resistance without becoming a horror omen.

## Placement and review

F5-B01 is offered soon after the first companion opportunity. F5-B02 and B03 compete for attention at the exchange; the player can try one now and the other later. B04 is a signed optional spur on the first road. No random encounter interrupts a shop, a family conversation or the public route. The representative pacing route can substitute one challenge for another rather than require every fight as a content checklist.

These fixtures provide opponents, objectives, intentions, legal options and recovery for a later build. They have not been playtested for enjoyment or timing. Preserve the distinction between a coherent paper decision, an appealing animated creature and a battle the player voluntarily wants again.
