# Opening creatures and commerce

This creature and spending contract serves [The First Five Hours](../02%20Narrative/First%20Five%20Hours.md). Individuals, scenes, prices and quantities remain **authored draft**. The [Bestiary](../01%20World/Bestiary.md) owns species ecology; [Adventure Rules](Adventure%20Rules%20v0.1.md) owns travel and recovery principles. The existing [five numerical kits](First%20Region%20Creature%20Kits.md) are unchanged.

The [detailed atlas](../01%20World/Rocca%20Selva%20Detailed%20Atlas.md) owns locations and `RS-I` interiors; creature records use `RS-C01–10`, shops `RS-SH01–06`, and goods `RS-IT01–22`. The main chapter owns wages and phases. [Rocca Residents and Side Stories](../02%20Narrative/Rocca%20Residents%20and%20Side%20Stories.md) owns `F5-S01–06`; completing those stories never silently recruits a creature. [Opening Battle Encounters](Opening%20Battle%20Encounters.md) owns the four opening battles, including RS-C10's optional wild confrontation.

## Encounters that survive a different route

The five phases are **Morning Arrival, Contract Accepted, Survey Settled, Booking Resolved, Evening Return**. Advancing requires a stated story commitment, never elapsed browsing time. Each record stores `.seen`, `.understood` and `.resolved` independently. An invitation additionally has `.ready`, `.joined` and `.declined`; declining preserves readiness for a later approach. Repeat scenes acknowledge the recorded choice rather than replaying a first meeting. Wildlife has no invisible affection percentage.

One or two companions are enough for the early outing. Invitations add a named individual to the collection, then offer **Travel together / Rest at a safe refuge**. Choosing the latter does not count as refusing the relationship. The player can retain a small active team even after meeting all five eligible individuals. Free suitable refuge access and keeper explanation accompany invitations; no device purchase, capture ball, meal or salve is a recruitment requirement. This opening's free creature loans are **supervised practice only**. Ordinary tools and human help cover every survey and booking requirement; someone with no owned companion can observe or pass RS-C10 peacefully instead of fighting.

## RS-C01 — Clatter has somewhere else to stand

At the arriving wagon, a horn tip and four planted feet interrupt the bustle. Clatter blocks the quiet ramp while people keep congratulating it for almost crossing. It is **Tavio's entrusted animal**, not unowned merchandise. Helping it does not authorize taking it. The whole arrival attracts attention even if the player never opens a creature menu; Tavio can introduce Pell from the wagon's other side.

Inspecting the ramp shows sound footing, a clapping spectator and an intrusive practice bell. Ask the crowd to stand back, walk the broad incline, or let Tavio postpone the crossing. All three resolve the immediate obstruction. No cue depends on hearing the bell: the visible horn vibration, posture and caption identify the distraction. Later, a short voluntary walk to Bell Court's rough post reveals the shoulder-press request and opens a separate invitation conversation.

**Tavio:** “He's crossed it twice. Then we formed a committee.”

**Protagonist:** “Does the committee need to clap?”

**Tavio:** “It appears to need that very much.”

**Protagonist:** “I'll take the long way. He can come if he wants.”

**Tavio:** “Good. And when you're done, Pell is hiring someone who notices things.”

**Tavio, after the walk:** “He came back for you. We can talk about traveling together.”

Accepting with Tavio's agreement sets `.joined`; Clatter follows, immediately stopping to scratch. Declining leaves it with Tavio and the same later invitation. Missing Morning Arrival moves the conversation to Tavio's evening tether-free rest area, with the broad walk still available. No practice victory is required. Its Bellwether kit and interruption identity remain ordinary trained possibilities, not a reward for solving the scene perfectly.

## RS-C02 — the most important broken tile

A trail of wet husks leads to the Gutter Lane drain. The Thimbleback is carrying the same nut uphill repeatedly because a shallow rivulet rolls it back. Its shell cups hold moss, three unremarkable stones and a blue tile. These are its possessions; the journal labels the cache before offering any take interaction.

Lift the loose side gate to redirect rainwater, or move its tray to the dry ledge using the free hook beside the drain. The carrying path reaches both points without jumping. A hop across the runnel offers a better view and a funny face-to-face encounter, not the exclusive solution. The animal immediately rescues its least valuable-looking object. On the return path it offers the chipped tile, retrieves it if placed down, and offers it again.

No resident must be at the drain. Inspection captions supply the complete observation: **“The rainwater rolls the nut back.” “The loose side gate opens a dry channel.” “The tray can also rest on that ledge.”** The repeated tile presentation then shows **“It offers you the tile again. Accept the gift or leave it beside the cache.”** Invitation readiness follows the chosen interaction, not a conversation elsewhere.

Later, **Ask about the tile collector** at Ruskell's actual chair-room or roof-garden location gives this optional exchange; never place a second Ruskell at the drain:

**Protagonist:** “Its cache is dry now. It saved one nut first.”

**Ruskell:** “An important nut, was it?”

**Protagonist:** “Looked just like the others.”

**Ruskell:** “That's why they don't ask us to sort them.”

**Protagonist:** “Then it kept offering me a tile.”

**Ruskell:** “You've been given something important too, then.”

Accepting the **gift** opens, but does not answer, the invitation. A player can keep the tile and leave the animal wild. `.resolved` places the dry cache at the edge of the Threshold Groves drying shed, RS-I12; later approaches begin there. Refusal produces a grave little pat of the tile and no penalty. The joined individual puts it beside the protagonist's boots at rest. Its resourceful physical disruption uses the existing Thimbleback kit; the cache contains no renewable coin reward.

## RS-C03 — a refuge with no job attached

Ollo's bakery and Sela's warm rooms share knowledge, not an unlimited furnace. During F5-S04, an adult Embermole avoids the drying screen's redirected sunlight, choosing the cool threshold. Ollo's backup stove keeps bread separate from this creature's comfort. Nobody must make it work again to finish the story.

After Survey Settled and either F5-S04's completion or Sela's own later arrangement, this independent adult visits an empty travel refuge in the court. A bent copper whisker and the smallest back plate's flutter identify it. Put the **provided** brick nearby, move the fancy cushion aside, or sit without offering anything. It chooses the hollow after those conditions are readable; no bought gift changes the result.

**Sela:** “That corner is allowed to be useless.”

**Protagonist:** “Ollo won't want the heat back?”

**Ollo:** “I want tomorrow's bread. The stove can help.”

**Sela:** “This one's an adult visitor. The young stay with their colony.”

**Protagonist:** “I've found it a very ugly brick.”

**Ollo:** “An excellent start to any friendship.”

Joining requires an explicit invitation after the animal settles. Leaving it there sets `.declined` and keeps its court return scene available. Sela keeps the refuge conditions suitable without recurring care errands. No early sign implies that domestic warmth is a trap. Its first field flourish can warm a damp seat; a cloth provides the same practical comfort. It contributes Embermole's existing Reach attacks, not free camp healing or unlimited industrial fuel.

## RS-C04 — three labels, one pair of boots

F5-S03 follows Neri's missing parcel to her rear porch off the carrying incline. A small Doorling has built a remarkably precise arrangement from loose labels and the laces of Neri's violet dancing boots. It does not belong to Tavio: his established companion remains his own animal, with a visibly different brow fold. This unattached individual has a cream notch in one outer fold and repeatedly lifts one foot before committing to a bad choice.

Resolve the parcel story by tracing its piles or comparing witness accounts and sorting labels. If the job was never accepted, Neri finding the parcel herself at closing also satisfies this condition, without paying an unearned fee. Once Survey Settled is reached, revisit the porch: the creature presents a hinge and places it at the protagonist's empty refuge. The journal states **“It is approaching your travel refuge. You can invite it.”** Earlier visits show ordinary sorting and name the later return opportunity.

**Neri:** “Those are my good boots.”

**Protagonist:** “They're purple all the way down.”

**Neri:** “Especially all the way down.”

**Protagonist:** “Can it keep the labels?”

**Neri:** “The blank ones. People remain entitled to their parcels.”

**Neri, at the invitation:** “That one's nobody's traveling companion yet. Ask it, not me.”

Invitation follows a separate choice; F5-S03's wage is identical either way. On decline, the Doorling drags the hinge back with offended concentration and remains approachable. On joining, it picks the hinge up and leaves the repaired shelter intact for wild neighbors. A first optional demonstration retrieves a dropped wooden counter through the porch slats; the nearby hook works too. Its battle removal remains the existing kit, never universal lock-opening or infinite storage.

## RS-C05 — the fair's strictest stage manager

Miret's practice cloth pulls free at Rope Walk. Look up before chasing it: a Kitefin is carefully folding the cloth over the wrong side of the lee rail, then correcting a streamer caught beneath itself. F5-S05 owns the borrowed-shade disagreement and three environmental clues. The animal is a familiar roost visitor, not Miret's property or the recruitable individual in the roster's later Cloud Loom encounter.

After the shelter is restored, choose **Offer a perch / Watch the landing / Leave the strip clear**. Each gives a distinct short flourish. With a perch it makes three unnecessary approach corrections; when watched it pretends the first failed landing was reconnaissance. This scene sets `.resolved` and a friendly return, not an invitation prompt.

**Miret:** “It has moved my chorus two feet to the left.”

**Protagonist:** “Better acoustics?”

**Miret:** “Better shade. Its standards are narrower than mine.”

**Protagonist:** “Will it come down?”

**Miret:** “It keeps choosing that rail. Let that be an answer.”

**Miret:** “There are others at Cloud Loom. Watch the streamers when the wind turns.”

Returning in Evening Return reveals its chosen perch above the quiet audience edge. Declining the side story leaves a clear temporary roost elsewhere, never a welfare failure caused by wandering. Its guide entry gives an actionable later lead: the Cloud Loom lee shelves, cloth-shaped shadows and a keeper familiar with the roosts. No human riding, numerical combat kit or first-hour full party follows from this sighting.

## RS-C06 — the animal that checks the masonry

Orla Dorr, a visiting quarry carrier, owns the familiar Cragmantle resting on Carrier Yard's sound limestone apron. Orla uses she/her. Its broad slate plates, warm belly and six opening foot pads are visible around a short hauling harness. It holds a **stationary hauling line** while people bring a modest load up the incline; it does not carry Tavio's entire wagon or cling to plaster walls.

Inspect the pad prints, offer to brush the dusty test stone with Orla's free brush, or watch from the marked resting side. After brushing, the animal taps, places a foot, then stops because Orla has not moved the line. Choosing **Follow its inspection** gives a brief ground-level route around three sound stones. The ordinary ramp parallels it; there is no mount unlock.

**Orla:** “Don't encourage it. Tell me which stone it rejected.”

**Protagonist:** “The polished one.”

**Orla:** “Dust under the polish. People call that a shortcut.”

**Protagonist:** “Does it ever hurry?”

**Orla:** “Toward its favorite boulder. Never away.”

**Orla:** “Old Switchback has wild ones. Learn the pads before you ask about riding.”

The bond is stated before interaction; **Invite** is absent, replaced by “Traveling with Orla.” `.resolved` records the footprint comparison. Declining permits a later demonstration through Evening Return. A borrowed brush cannot be sold. The later guide lead distinguishes cleaned gorge walls and fresh seep marks after rain from supernatural stone memory. This is an aspirational meeting, not a purchasable early mount.

## RS-C07 — four wings beyond the roofs

After Survey Settled, the pass-house porch frames an enormous unfolding silhouette on the exterior rest ledge, RS-08c. Eren Voss, a Cloud Loom courier who uses he/him, has landed his own Sailrook. The public approach ends behind a low rail; its landing shelf, wing clearance and thermal corridor lie beyond village streets. A handbill at the porch also points to the view if the player approaches by the incline.

Choose **Watch the wing check / Ask about the route / Return later**. The wing check reveals four separately folded wings; the bird drops a tough seed husk and catches it, while Eren tries to look professional. Selecting **Watch departure** confirms the short scene. There is no timer requiring the player to race here; departure waits for that choice or the explicit next-day transition.

**Eren:** “I brought one letter. She's brought half a hillside.”

**Protagonist:** “Can she take off from here?”

**Eren:** “From that ledge, into that rising air. Not your kitchen roof.”

**Protagonist:** “Where does the path under you go?”

**Eren:** “Same pass. More corners.”

**Eren:** “Cloud Loom has a public rest shelf. Ask there before approaching a nest.”

Its existing bond excludes invitation or spontaneous riding. Missing departure leaves Eren's route sketch and the same safe later lead, not a failed collection. `.resolved` records either the observed wing check or route conversation; `.departure_seen` separately preserves whether the player watched the glide. No feather is forcibly harvested and no flight statistics are added.

## RS-C08 — the stone with the correct lump

Pell's White Orchard booking reaches a public herd path, where Vela is recording access around feeding Glassgrazers. Ressa, the local herd attendant, uses she/her and remains the authority on which individual can leave. One independent adult has a naturally scalloped outer fan and insists on approaching an awkward stone from both sides. Its soft face and six legs remain visible beneath the mineral grandeur.

Ressa's **public scratch-stone path** is independent of the paid demonstration's rig and canopy approach. Walk with the willing adult along it, use the parallel accessible path while Ressa escorts it, or leave the meeting for later. Choosing the old footway or withdrawing from Pell's demonstration does not prevent this public encounter. At the stone, its repeated sideways presentation asks for space to turn; moving the loose empty basket supplies it. If Vela is present, she records what happened rather than treating a scratched fan as evidence of illness; otherwise Ressa supplies the same observed facts without inventing Vela's presence.

**If Vela is present:**

**Vela:** “I drew a magnificent diagram of the obstruction.”

**Ressa:** “It's the basket.”

**Protagonist:** “The stone isn't too narrow?”

**Ressa:** “The stone is perfect. We have this argument every week.”

**Vela:** “I shall label the diagram 'basket.'”

**Ressa, after the return:** “That adult sometimes travels. We can prepare a refuge if it wants yours.”

**If Vela is absent, use this complete exchange instead:**

**Ressa:** “It comes here for the lump on that stone.”

**Protagonist:** “Then why does it keep backing away?”

**Ressa:** “The basket's in its turning space.”

**Protagonist:** “I'll move it.”

**Ressa:** “There. Now the other side.”

**Ressa, after the return:** “That adult sometimes travels. We can prepare a refuge if it wants yours.”

The invitation follows this public walk, independently of Pell's paid work. Ressa explains suitable rest and herd contact before `.ready`. Declining returns the animal to its herd with a repeatable visit. Tavio-route or no-booking players receive Ressa's public visiting directions after settlement; this encounter remains for another local outing, without awarding Pell's unworked wage. Glassgrazer uses its existing durable rescue kit. Its invitation does not require taking Vela's authorship position or purchasing ornamental minerals.

## RS-C09 — something is watching the ordinary path

At the outer Threshold Groves fence, a fresh scrape, clipped hare fur and a quiet patch of scrub lead away from the maintained path. The guide first says **“A hunting animal uses the cover beyond this fence.”** Choosing **Observe from the gate** reveals a Vergecat breathing in shade; a small change of viewpoint resolves the apparently misplaced shoulder into its whole body. A visible caption communicates the movement.

The unattended observation supplies captions: **“Fresh hare fur catches on the low branch.” “One shoulder moves with the animal's breathing.” “The hare track turns into cover; the public path follows the fence.”** These establish the animal and ordinary alternative without Dema. The journal marks the upper curve after inspection.

After this sighting, **Ask Dema about the grove animal** is available at her actual market stall or roof-garden location. She looks at the recorded sketch; she is not spawned at the boundary:

**Dema:** “That's the grove fence. Keep to the public side.”

**Protagonist:** “I thought there were two.”

**Dema:** “Next time, watch the shadow, then the shoulder.”

**Protagonist:** “Will it follow the path?”

**Dema:** “I'd expect it to follow the hare run. We can use the gate.”

**Dema:** “Take the upper curve. You can see the whole terrace from there.”

Walk the ordinary curve, observe once, or return to town. All preserve survey access. The animal is not secretly teleported behind the player, and no menu choice starts an unannounced battle. `.resolved` marks the recorded boundary and overlook; there is no invitation. A later supervised Staired Wood encounter can develop its prepared pounce, cover requirements and nursing boundaries. The opening glimpse adds wonder and ecological danger without announcing the story's horror or implying every animal exists to join the party.

[F5-B04](Opening%20Battle%20Encounters.md#f5-b04--rs-c10--the-owner-of-the-short-path) owns this encounter's warning, free pebble approach, observation route, declared 18-HP wild fixture and recovery. ## Shops with something to say

All listed stock is **remaining stock for this whole opening visit**, consumed only by completed purchases; phase changes do not refill it. Shops show item, price, exact effect, quantity and purse before confirmation. Unused goods can be returned to the selling counter for the exact price during this visit, restoring its stock; there is no resale-profit loop. Gifts, loans and used consumables cannot be sold. A proprietor away on a scene leaves a location note and an interaction to call them to the counter; essentials never depend on a real-time schedule.

### RS-SH01 — Bera's repair bay, RS-I07, Carrier Yard

Bera sells durable tools and handles F5-S02's cart work. The band around a new pole is stamped with her mark; a repaired wheel, chalked with measurements, supplies visual evidence of actual work. **Free:** inspect the commission's loan kit, tighten its fittings, borrow/return required tools. **Optional service:** 3 coins to mend the visible tear in the protagonist's old work outfit; purely cosmetic, once, with preview. No equipment durability meter is introduced.

**Bera:** “Buying, borrowing, or hoping I'll praise that handle?”

**Protagonist:** “Borrowing first.”

**Bera:** “Sensible. The pass-house rack. Yours to use while you're working.”

**Protagonist:** “And this one?”

**Bera:** “Six coins. Your own. It can be ugly on your behalf for years.”

**Protagonist:** “A promising career.”

**Ollo, before the commission:** “If you've packed lunch, these are the unnecessary ones.”

**Ollo, once the commission is active or settled:** “Paid lunch is covered. These are the unnecessary ones.”

**Protagonist:** “The unnecessary ones look better.”

**Ollo, before the commission:** “They have to. You already have lunch.”

**Ollo, once the commission is active or settled:** “They have to. Lunch has a contract.”

### RS-SH04 — Tavio's cloth and medicine, RS-05a, Carrier Yard

### RS-SH06 — Dema's herb stall, RS-04a, Bell Court

## Use the thing you chose

The following are the opening's complete small purchase payoffs. Inspecting or previewing consumes nothing. Confirmation applies the printed use once; skipping its animation does not repeat or cancel the transaction. Recipients appear only at their actual recorded location. With nobody appropriate present, **Keep for later** remains available; no shared-food scene summons an absent family member. These scenes grant no hidden affection, growth or repeated money.
