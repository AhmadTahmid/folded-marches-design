# Bells Before Breakfast

**Role:** first morning and the road-opening fair in Rocca Selva. **Current authority:** [First Five Hours](../First%20Five%20Hours.md), [Opening Day Script](../Opening%20Day%20Script.md), and [Rocca Residents and Side Stories](../Rocca%20Residents%20and%20Side%20Stories.md). The earlier three-obligation stewardship and remote-saddle fair are superseded opening proposals; their historical version remains in the original public v0.1 snapshot. No hidden errands survive as progression conditions.

## The opening promise

The protagonist is nineteen in the working draft, already helping support his mother and three younger siblings. Today the roads reopen. He wants paid field work, a reference and the chance to see places whose goods he normally carries. His family is alive, occupied and capable of disagreeing. The opening does not explain the absent father, burn the village or burden the player with a survival timer.

Morning starts with the good shirt serving as Nella's roof, Ada finishing a harness and the sound of Tavio's awkward arrival. The objective is concrete: find paid road work at Carrier Yard. The wagon's goods, a magnificent owned creature and a visible little commotion make the destination worth approaching before anybody explains the world.

## Playable sequence

1. **Leave a real household.** Take lunch and the bag; choose an attitude without selecting a different family. Children continue their own activities after the scene.
2. **Enter a working market.** Tavio and Pavo have a cart problem that other workers can solve if the player watches. Tavio introduces Pell regardless of whether helped. The player can meet Pell directly.
3. **Notice a partner.** Clatter refuses a noisy crossing. Make room, open the wide gate, watch or leave. A later keeper-agreed invitation follows behavior; no creature is payment for an errand. Other local species have independent meetings.
4. **Understand a paid job.** Pell shows three observations, a report destination and the total pay. Ada reviews the terms at her nearby market table or home fallback. Accepting starts the survey and pays its stated advance once.
5. **Choose how much home to explore.** Shops, rooms, smith work, neighbors, creature care and side stories remain optional. Mara offers a contest for pleasure, not a gate to employment. The first road stays open.

The morning beat completes when the job is accepted or the player deliberately remains in local exploration. The paid commission completes separately at the exchange. The next wider destination is chosen after the report or evening return. No species, combat victory, table delivery, cloth rescue or parcel story is compulsory.

## Ordinary surprises before horror

The fair occupies Bell Court, Carrier Yard and inhabited connecting streets. Its upper views extend toward Rope Walk and the pass house; another detached fair map is not required. Arriving stock, moving performances, an unexpected opened room and an owned aerial creature create surprise. The marker discrepancy belongs to the survey, with both numerals preserved and no compulsory ominous interpretation.

The older flour substitution idea is retained as ordinary taste in the sour-chestnut food and the later [Feast for an Empty Place](Feast%20for%20an%20Empty%20Place.md). That later quest must work even if no early parcel dispute occurred; it does not pretend the player made a deleted choice. The wrapped answering-bell anomaly is not an additional required opening event.

## Departure and return

Declining invitations leaves people and animals with their stated lives. Optional stories specify independent resolution and later re-entry. The survey preserves observations through retreat. The village changes at authored journey phases, not because the player read slowly. The evening can contain payment acknowledgment, a true account to the family, a short performance and a new destination.
