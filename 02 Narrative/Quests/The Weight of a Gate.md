# The Weight of a Gate

**Role:** major Orthe arc; the material cost of refusal, public safety, unequal access. **Place:** Orthe's Counterweight District, Threshold Court, and Hall of Measures. **People:** Magistrate Neris Vale and Iona Serr; a small group of applicants whose distinct identities should be developed in a later character pass. See [Orthe](../../01%20World/Orthe.md).

**Prerequisite:** arrival in Orthe or a request to investigate its interrupted water traffic. **Completion:** an accountable water and travel arrangement is established, with the applicants' preferences recorded and supported. The city need not abandon its limits to resolve the case.

## The city that can explain its machines

Orthe's Counterweight District is beautiful in a different way from Istravel. Towers of pale stone contain open weights that descend through public squares. Children follow painted lines on the paving to see which cistern a wheel feeds. Iona can point at almost every part and name someone who knows how to repair it. This legibility is a real achievement.

At Threshold Court, people arriving from stronger accords can receive transition support. A maintenance crisis has interrupted that support. Several applicants cannot safely proceed into Orthe without it; their onward transfer is also delayed because the same overloaded office must certify portable care arrangements. One wants to enter, one wants to return to Istravel, and one wants to reach the Reedhouse. Treating them as a single “refugee problem” already misses the point.

Neris has not invented the danger. A previous poorly supported withdrawal injured people. Iona believes the present rules treat every exception as a possible precedent until no exception is fast enough to matter.

## Scene sequence

**1. Follow the weight.** The protagonist accompanies Iona from an immense descending stone through the district's water system. Embermoles reclaim heat from mechanical workshops; a bounded Ribbonwake accord helps at one difficult change in elevation. The system is mixed. Orthe's independence is a practice of limiting and maintaining dependencies, not an absence of all magic.

**2. Meet people before their files.** Each applicant has a destination and a boundary. The person seeking entry may accept a slower transition but reject public examination. The person returning to Istravel is not conceding a philosophical argument; their child or vocation is there. The person seeking the Reedhouse wishes to speak to a transformed friend without promising to transform themselves.

**3. The closed sluice.** A protective gate has been fixed shut after a measurement failure. Opening it under existing conditions risks flooding a low workshop. Keeping it shut preserves the buildings but withholds a supply line needed for transition care. Iona finds a manual bypass, physically plausible within the fiction, that needs workers, replacement seals, and a negotiated use of a reserve cistern. No single magical animal can erase all three requirements.

**4. A hearing with a body in it.** Neris invites testimony in the Hall of Measures. The official timetable assumes applicants can wait where they are; one applicant explains that waiting itself changes their ability to communicate. The protagonist can present the case through a representative, arrange remote or written testimony, or seek a provisional decision. Requiring a dramatic collapse to prove the problem would repeat the failure under investigation.

**5. A public choice about who carries inconvenience.** The reserve cistern serves a prestigious restoration project and a modest washhouse. Equal percentage cuts would burden their users differently. Iona proposes delaying ornamental work; Neris can defend doing so if the evidence and maintenance plan withstand public scrutiny. Local workers need payment or reciprocal support, not an inspirational speech that makes labor free.

## Resolution paths

- **Repair and create a bounded exception.** Secure the bypass and a time-limited portable-care permit. This helps the applicants without removing general limits. Its weakness is repetition: future applicants still need a process, and an exception that depends on a traveling hero is fragile.
- **Fund a standing transition service.** Build a coalition for the reserve allocation, staff training, and an appeal route. This produces broader benefit but demands sustained civic commitments. Istravel assistance can help, provided Orthe retains inspectable terms and a way to end the arrangement.
- **Support distinct onward journeys.** Some applicants choose to leave. Arrange viable travel and receiving care with existing caravans and institutions. This can fully honor their wishes while leaving Orthe's structural exclusion insufficiently changed; the epilogue should say both things.
- **Accept a temporary restrictive ruling.** If the current evidence does not justify a safe opening, the protagonist can support the ruling while securing care and a prompt review. This is different from declaring the delay harmless. Refusing all support leaves an unresolved case, not a completed rescue.

Forging certification or forcing the gate can be a deliberately risky narrative branch if later scope permits. It should cause understandable consequences and repair opportunities; it is not the automatic “free people” answer. The first draft does not require a stealth or sabotage system.

## A subtle echo of the larger mystery

One form repeats “no unaddressed preference remains” despite three applicants plainly asking for different outcomes. This could be bureaucratic compression. If the protagonist compares the form's accord-assisted summary with the original testimony, a substantive phrase has been replaced by a plausible administrative category. The original document survives. Whether a clerk or an adaptive process introduced the change can be investigated; do not declare that all human bureaucracy is secretly supernatural.

## Order, abort, and reentry

Coming from Istravel supplies practical knowledge of care dependence. Coming here first makes the city's caution understandable before the player sees a garden's failure. Confluence testimony can help identify reversible support, but cannot speak for the applicants.

If the player leaves before a decision, Neris institutes temporary care and a recorded review date measured by story events, not real-world hours. The case remains pending. An abrupt manual intervention can cause a localized emergency and temporarily close that route; an alternate entrance and repair hearing prevent a narrative dead end. On reentry, Iona explains what the city did in the protagonist's absence without resolving every disputed preference for them.
