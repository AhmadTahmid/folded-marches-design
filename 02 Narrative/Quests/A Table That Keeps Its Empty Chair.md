# A Table That Keeps Its Empty Chair

**Role:** cross-faction culmination of the proposed first major story; an expedition, an assembly, and a material settlement. **Place:** a shared water-and-road junction on the Braided Road; its exact architecture and name remain open. **People:** invited representatives from Istravel, Orthe, the Confluence, mountain communities, and affected travelers; their presence depends on relationships and consent. **Creatures:** local Ribbonwake, working and wild companions, and species affected by the junction's footprint.

**Prerequisite:** evidence of a repeatable discrepancy, testimony from an affected person, and contact with at least two communities. A third community can send an invited witness without requiring every regional quest. **Completion:** a regional or explicitly limited local arrangement is enacted with a known maintenance plan, stated beneficiaries, and acknowledged unresolved interests. Choosing a cosmological explanation is unnecessary.

## The promise on the table

The proposed shared accord could make devastating seasonal shortages rare. It could keep the Highwater Arcade flowing during drought, support transition care at Orthe, and protect mountain roads without requiring every settlement to negotiate alone. Its advocates have concrete people in mind.

The failures the protagonist has witnessed suggest a danger in declaring many unlike needs equivalent. Before a wide connection is completed, an assembly is called at the junction. The protagonist can bring evidence, introductions, and practical options. They are an influential participant, not the sovereign empowered to decide everyone's future by selecting a final dialogue color.

## Preparation: four visible commitments

The journal records actual arrangements, each with named contributors and limitations:

1. **Care:** who keeps people alive and supported during a transition, interruption, or refusal?
2. **Exit:** which routes and services allow a community or individual to leave without forfeiting basic survival?
3. **Independent account:** who retains evidence and can challenge an official summary without relying on the same process being challenged?
4. **Unrepresented interests:** how are animals, nonparticipants, future arrivals, and people unable to attend considered, without claiming to know every preference on their behalf?

These are questions with concrete answers, not four abstract meters. Senna may provide a treatment arrangement; Iona a repairable alternative; Neris a bounded authorization; Arel resources and public accountability; Corda a plural testimony practice. Local alternatives exist if one refuses. A participant's friendship with the protagonist is not permission to volunteer their labor indefinitely.

## Preparation expedition: the road that welcomes

Before the broad connection begins, the proposed junction opens an astonishing shortcut. A party can cross miles of difficult lowland through a succession of sheltered terraces, each arriving with the water, shade, or rest place they were about to need. The protagonist accompanies willing witnesses carrying independently recorded accounts. Their physical objective is to verify a retreat route that can support people and creatures whose care needs differ.

The first half is exhilarating. A Ribbonwake current carries small supply barges between shallow pools; overhead, Kitefin follow newly formed warm updrafts. A wild Doorling has appropriated a loose official ribbon for its nest. The route is useful and the people are happy to be spared an exhausting journey.

Then a witness wants to return to collect something the route's guide considers replaceable. The exit markers now lead to increasingly suitable substitutes: a new instrument, an excellent blanket, an annotated copy of a letter. The original destination is still shown on the map, but each convenient turning leads toward the assembly. Refusal has not been forbidden in words; it has become physically difficult to enact.

The party stops treating comfort as evidence of direction. They compare a measured cord, water flow, old service marks, and animal behavior. A companion may want a familiar dry ledge that the new route considers inefficient. Wild animals can reveal another path, but their movements are clues rather than guaranteed rescue instructions. The player can survey this less accommodating route with borrowed equipment even without a suitable companion.

The climax is a traverse through an unfinished embankment while the inviting path continues offering increasingly personal reasons to turn back. A rope team preserves the way across loose footing; separate witnesses keep separate accounts; a carried record cannot be allowed to become the sole irreplaceable account. A Doorling may attempt to steal a tag at the worst possible moment, remaining a living nuisance rather than an oracle. Taking the rough exit returns the party to a known place whose ordinary weather feels unexpectedly immense.

**Proposed personal ordeal:** [The breath that agrees](../Horror%20and%20Fourth%20Wall.md#an-ordeal-the-protagonist-carries-out) places a direct bodily encounter inside this traverse. The protagonist struggles to speak while the terrace projects their reassuring voice; allies recognize the mismatch and use the established rope signals to help the group across the boundary. The player retains meaningful actions and a usable retreat. A subsequent recovery scene and changed work arrangements from [Personal Story](../Personal%20Story.md) give the event consequences beyond evidence for the assembly. This replaces no actual player choice with invented negligence, and does not require a particular catch or identity option.

This supplies direct evidence that the proposed connection cannot yet guarantee an exit. It also produces a surveyed alternative. A party that retreats early can send a specialist team or argue for a smaller initial connection; the assembly acknowledges the weaker evidence and unfinished route. There is no secret rule that choosing the comfortable path makes the player morally complicit forever.

This sequence instantiates one of the shared, origin-neutral spectacles in [Cosmic Possibilities](../Cosmic%20Possibilities.md). Its geography should be frighteningly capable and locally testable, not an excuse to teleport the player arbitrarily whenever the plot needs them elsewhere.

## Scene sequence

**1. The approach.** The junction resembles an unfinished city reflected in a river that has not decided which way to flow. Stairs rise toward the forms of buildings promised by different plans, then become banks of reeds. It is spectacular before it is frightening. Delegates arrive carrying ordinary things: a meal, an accessible chair, a maintenance ledger, a creature's bedding.

**2. A successful demonstration.** A brief, consensually bounded connection corrects a shortage and relieves an actual person's discomfort. Let the player see why abandoning the work has a cost. Then the meeting minutes describe agreement on a point several delegates have just disputed. Their original statements survive in independent records if these were arranged; otherwise witnesses can still challenge the summary, with less evidentiary security.

**3. The offered settlement.** The accord's interpreter presents an elegant account in which every faction achieves what it values. Orthe's independence becomes unnecessary because the shared system guarantees repair. Istravel's universal provision excludes nobody because nobody wishes to refuse. Confluence pluralism is celebrated because every internal voice eventually harmonizes. Each ideal has been preserved in language while losing a hard practical condition.

The interpreter may be an office, a creature-mediated ritual, or a partly responsive place; its final form remains open. It does not deliver a villain speech explaining all reality. Asked what happens to someone who still disagrees, it may answer, “Then we have not finished helping.”

**4. A junction under strain.** Keeping incompatible accounts present disrupts the proposed synchronization. Water paths diverge; platforms become difficult to cross; wild Ribbonwake follow newly opened currents. The player and allies must carry people and records to stable ground, slow or partition the connection, and keep a chosen channel open. This is a proposed expedition climax, not a commitment to a particular combat or simulation system.

Creature companions help according to established behavior. They are not sacrificed as universal keys. A companion may refuse an unsafe task; the plan needs another route, more time, or assistance. The player can decline a spectacular maneuver without losing the only legitimate ending.

**5. A chair for refusal.** The assembly returns to a smaller table on stable ground. One place remains unassigned. Its purpose is procedural: any settlement must describe what happens when a person arrives with a request it cannot satisfy. An empty chair is a memorable image, but the real safeguard is the funded route, retained testimony, independent advocate, or authority to stop a local connection.

**6. Make a settlement and show its first cost.** Arel announces a delayed expansion, Neris accepts an inspectable external support agreement, Corda withdraws from one obligation, or a mountain community takes responsibility for a difficult route. The result becomes believable because someone acts on it immediately.

## The expedition inside the junction

The assembly is the social frame; the middle of the climax should be a substantial, dangerous journey through a place the participants do not control. The following sequence gives the creature RPG a tangible culmination without selecting a final origin. The alien scale can later draw on [Cosmic Possibilities](../Cosmic%20Possibilities.md).

**The hanging nursery.** The refuge route crosses a loop of water suspended above a stone gallery. Wild Ribbonwake are sheltering young in its slow inner current; the new connection has begun pulling that nursery toward a fast channel. A local keeper can coordinate a short diversion while companions and delegates ferry light equipment across the dry ledge. A Kitefin can carry a guide cord, never a human passenger. A player without that companion uses a longer stair and a borrowed throwing line. Saving the nursery preserves a stable crossing; treating the animals as obstacles can make the route much less predictable without triggering supernatural revenge.

**The white workshop.** A maintenance room is filled with beautifully intact furniture above a floor of severed straps. Velvet Undoers are consuming damp glues, lacquer, and stored fibers. Their plush bodies turn slowly toward newly wetted materials. This is material danger: wet record bindings, harnesses, and emergency stretchers can fail. Dry stone shelves and a route through the older masonry offer refuge. A Thimbleback may locate a familiar maintenance cache by scent; a human can find it through the surviving service marks. The player must choose what to carry first and arrange a second recovery if possible. The creature does not literally eat memories, even when the object at risk is a cherished record.

**The underside of the river.** Beyond the workshop, the water appears to continue through a volume far larger than the valley. Distant light opens in rows like windows, and each opening seems to contain this same expedition making a slightly different choice. A dropped stone returns an echo before it strikes. No one can establish whether these are places, predictions, reflected memories, or an unfamiliar organism's structures. An animal beside the protagonist continues to demand a very ordinary response: steady footing, breathing room, a hand kept still. That small known relationship gives the scene an emotional center while its scale exceeds explanation.

**A predator displaced.** A Vergecat has been driven from a dry den into the service approaches. It prepares pounces from the same cover-edge behavior established in earlier encounters. The uncanniness of the place does not make its reach arbitrary. The group can wait for an opening, use another path, protect an exposed crossing, or engage it if later combat design supports that choice. It is hungry and frightened, not the source's ordained guardian. A careful player can understand the threat even while the wider place remains incomprehensible.

**The return signal.** The final route back requires keeping a physical passage recognizable as currents and local folds shift. Bellwether calls can link the group through fog; ordinary bells, visible markers, and paired guides provide alternatives. A refusing companion is not punished or overwritten. The party partitions the connection through accessible local controls and a maintained alternate channel; it does not defeat the cosmos by pulling a convenient single lever. The result is a survivable local boundary and enough time to return to the assembly with witnesses.

The expedition should leave marks that the later settlement must account for: a nursery protected or displaced, a route abandoned, records recovered or partly lost, a delegate who experienced something another did not. Its most disturbing implication is that the regional junction may be only a tiny expression of a much larger process. Its definite accomplishment is bringing particular lives and disagreements back out of it.

## Outcome construction

The [Story Architecture](../Story%20Architecture.md) names five possible emphases: Open Compact, Great Provision, Separate Hearths, Living Treaty, and Long Refusal. The final arrangement can combine them by place. The assembly cannot promise unsupported universal freedom, and the narrative should not label every limitation a failure.

| Established conditions | A credible resulting behavior |
|---|---|
| Strong independent care and exit routes; several communities commit maintenance | Wider connections can remain voluntarily revisable, at a cost in speed and labor |
| Broad provision, weak alternatives, but honest disclosure and continuing advocates | Integration improves lives while leaving a contested dependency the epilogue follows |
| Strong local capacity, limited trust between cities | Smaller accords persist; cross-border emergency aid becomes the central unfinished duty |
| Voluntary embodied mediators with protected withdrawal and succession | A Living Treaty can develop without making transformed people permanent infrastructure |
| A coalition rejects connection and has credible replacement logistics | The shared proposal is retired while existing services are preserved and gradually renegotiated |
| No viable broad coalition | A narrower local settlement prevents an immediate crisis; the wider agreement remains contested rather than magically resolved |

Knowledge matters because it improves proposals. Relationships matter because people may trust a process enough to participate. Neither knowledge nor affection can override an explicit refusal. There is no hidden “most human” score selecting the true ending.

## Optional fourth-wall culmination

After repeated in-fiction encounters with recorded accounts, the interpreter may address the observer who returns to the page. It offers a final version “without the unfinished places.” The protagonist can preserve the incomplete account, accept a proposed summary while retaining dissent, or refuse the exchange.

The effect refers only to earlier in-game text and choices. The actual save, menu, device, and player identity remain outside its reach. If direct address is reduced, the same argument occurs through a fictional archivist reading the page. Declining the effect cannot cost a companion or an ending. See [Horror and Fourth Wall](../Horror%20and%20Fourth%20Wall.md).

## Discovery order, postponement, and reentry

The player may call for more evidence or postpone the broad vote. Delegates state what can safely continue in the meantime. A preview of the proposed settlement identifies missing care or exit arrangements before commitment; it does not simply warn of an unspecified point of no return.

The junction sequence has explicit in-fiction preparation and a supported retreat route. Leaving before it begins keeps the preparation state. Leaving during it becomes a retreat with a bounded provisional settlement and a chance to reconvene, subject to the people affected; it does not reset the scene as though nobody remembers.

If optional quests were missed, delegates raise ecological or mountain interests through available witnesses. Their proposals are less detailed and may need later review. If a regional investigation ended badly, its people can send a critical witness or refuse attendance. The finale acknowledges that limit rather than silently replacing them with an identical loyal substitute.

## Epilogue requirements

Show at least one beneficiary, one person bearing a cost, one dissenter, one companion relationship, and the protagonist's evolving connection to Rocca Selva. Preserve a joy that was never secretly a trick. The amount of secure comfort follows the tonal direction eventually chosen with the user; it is not selected by this quest draft.

The cosmic source remains open. The regional outcome does not. The player should know which bridge is maintained, who can cross it, and whom to ask when the agreement fails.
