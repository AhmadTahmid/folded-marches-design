# A Bell Above the Clouds

**Role:** optional mountain expedition; Jori's competence and ambition; a shared success with a lasting return scene. **Place:** a keeper shelter on the accessible [Cloud Loom](../../01%20World/Atlas.md#the-cloud-loom) approach. **People:** Jori Tern, local keepers, optional Mara introduction. **Creatures:** wild Kitefin, optional Bellwether or other appropriate partners. **Prerequisite:** regional access and a safe announced weather interval. **Completion:** deliver and mount an ordinary replacement signal bell, or agree and perform a workable temporary alternative.

## The job before the player arrives

The shelter's old bell has a cracked attachment. Its keepers use a hand signal and a runner while it is out of service. A replacement waits below; the existing path and small service hoist require inspection before lifting it. Nobody is secretly dying while the player explores.

Jori has accepted the freight job and wants a second outcome: an evening audience for his short comedy about a merchant who rents the same donkey to three customers. He asks the protagonist for paid help. The handling terms are visible before departure. If they met during Tavio's wagon problem, he remembers the work; otherwise he introduces himself through the job, with no invented shared adventure.

The invitation can come from Mara, the keeper's notice, or finding Jori at the departure shelter. Declining means the keepers hire another pair. The route and future performance remain visitable.

## An ascent with choices

**Pack below the ridge.** Jori can balance an awkward load much better than his joking manner suggests. He takes one of the protagonist's clever knots apart and explains what would happen under sideways strain. Then he tries to add a painted theatre panel to the shared pack. The player can accept the extra carry for an agreed fee, ask him to carry it himself, or suggest performing without scenery. He can be mildly offended and still keep the bargain.

**Choose the approach.** The maintained switchback permits carrying teams and ordinary rests. An inspected stone traverse shortens part of the route but requires the supplied line and an empty hand. A suitable climbing mount can help with a small delivery stage, within its species limits; it does not carry an unsecured bell up an arbitrary wall. Both paths reach the lower hoist landing, with different views and conversations. Ownership of a specialist is unnecessary.

**Look before lifting.** Kitefin shelter on one side of the saddle before a gust. Loose marker cloth and a keeper's forecast confirm the change; the animal is one source of evidence. The player can choose the next announced calm interval directly at shelter. Waiting is an authored transition, not repeated weather rolls.

## A real limit and a satisfying alternative

Inspection may reveal that the preferred mounting beam needs repair. This authored finding is disclosed before committing the load, never a punishment for a previous dialogue answer. The player and Jori choose between the usable freestanding support, which preserves most of the viewing platform, and a temporary lower post plus a clear runner arrangement until the beam is repaired. Both use supplied materials and keeper labor, not a crafting tree.

The first option completes a longer work session and postpones Jori's performance until another visit. The second finishes sooner and keeps tonight's small show, while the keepers accept a specific follow-up repair. Pay covers the agreed work in either case; the difference is a durable installation now or a staffed temporary arrangement. Neither relies on someone working indefinitely for free. Jori can state which he prefers without turning it into a test of loyalty.

## Complete the lift

**Raise the bell together.** Jori wants to keep the guide line and work the hoist because explaining his preferred rhythm feels slower. The protagonist can insist on dividing roles, ask a keeper to lead, or agree a slower staged lift. A small Kitefin can carry a light messenger line between nearby posts; humans use it to draw the proper working line across. It never lifts the bell or a person. Without a creature partner, a keeper takes the line by the maintained footway.

This is a readable sequence of preparation choices, not an unannounced reflex test. Stopping at any stage leaves the load secured. The finished bell has a visible suspension and ordinary uses; it does not become a supernatural instrument because it mattered in a quest.

## The evening people remember

The colored veils spread above the saddle. If the show proceeds, the protagonist can join a small part, remain in the audience, or spend the interval watching creatures from the platform. Jori's merchant voice is excellent; his donkey is terrible. A keeper laughs at the wrong line and improves the rest of the act by arguing with it.

If the show is postponed, Jori performs a minute of it while the pair eats after work. He is disappointed because the audience mattered, not because he expects the protagonist to manage his happiness. A later invitation makes the full show available. The scenery choice appears: painted panel, a borrowed cloth, or Jori pointing at an imaginary grand stable.

## Consequences and recurrence

Return visits show the chosen bell arrangement and the eventual repair. Jori later negotiates a job differently if he learned to share the lift; if the player never challenged him, he can learn through another colleague, without falsely crediting the protagonist. His stage ambition advances through bookings and practice, not a single affection threshold.

The rewards are agreed pay, a useful route, a performance or invitation, a keeper connection, and recognition of creature/weather signs. Ordinary retreat leads to a safe landing; interrupted work is secured by the crew and can resume. No failed action breaks the household support plan or permanently bars a recruit.

**Information record:** the attachment, beam, forecast, crew agreement, and installed support are practical facts. Later use of group signals can recall this shared work, but the bell offers no secret protection against the Largesse. Its exact repair staging and interface remain design proposals.
