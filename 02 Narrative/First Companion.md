# The creature that comes with him

**Developed example, not a compulsory starter:** the inexperienced [Bellwether](../01%20World/Bestiary.md#bellwether) from [Bells Before Breakfast](Quests/Bells%20Before%20Breakfast.md). The working nickname is **Clatter**; the player can choose another. Its appearance, history, and scenes below remain proposals. The protagonist is the young provider established in [Personal Story](Personal%20Story.md), with a mother and three younger siblings. His father's absence stays an open author decision.

The relationship should begin with two creatures wanting an interesting road. He wants work and a wider life. Clatter wants room to move, familiar calls answered, and access to particular kinds of scrub. Neither understands the other's ambitions completely. They can nevertheless become very good at traveling together.

## An individual before a symbol

Clatter has a pale crescent beneath one eye, broad feet, and a horn that produces a dry rasp before its clear note. It loves rubbing its neck against coarse upright surfaces. It will ignore a beautifully padded resting place to stand beside an ugly brushwood post. Raised viewpoints attract it; low ceilings make it choose a place nearer the door.

Its characteristic request is two little shoulder presses followed by a backward step. Depending on context, it wants room to pass or wants someone to move a scratching hand. The movement is recognizable without always meaning the same thing. Captions and visible posture accompany its sounds.

It enjoys wind through its horns. After finding a satisfying angle, it sometimes turns too far, loses the note, and indignantly turns back. A wet rope has to be smelled at insulting length. These habits continue when the plot becomes frightening.

This is the fair animal that freezes during applause, **not automatically the unbeaten challenge animal** in [Companionship and Catching](../03%20Game%20Design/Companionship%20and%20Catching.md). They demonstrate different individuals within one species. Clatter's attention to a pause between calls can later support ordinary training in interrupting; it gives no exclusive ability or numerical advantage.

## First meeting: stop helping so loudly

The plank is short. The animal crossed it before the spectators arrived. Now everybody has advice.

“Encourage it,” someone says.

The protagonist tries a soft call. Three people repeat it, louder. Clatter plants all four feet and looks behind itself. Its horn answers the nearby fair bell; applause interrupts the quiet in which it would listen for a response.

The player can ask for space, try the wider path, investigate the bell, or postpone. In the quiet approach, the protagonist moves beside the plank instead of dragging from the far end. Clatter follows, stops halfway to rub against a post, and completes the crossing when everyone has become bored enough to look elsewhere.

Tavio starts congratulating him. Clatter walks between them and asks Tavio for a scratch. It has not instantly transferred every attachment to a stranger.

Later, away from the demonstration, the protagonist offers an ordinary companionship invitation with the keeper's agreement. He chooses the slower return path. Clatter follows for several bends, goes back to inspect Tavio's wagon, then catches up. Familiar handling, interesting terrain, and the chance to choose distance explain its approach. No animal recognizes his destiny or judges his worthiness to provide for a family.

## First friction: he mistakes cooperation for speed

Before the survey, he practices the crossing repeatedly. Each successful attempt seems like evidence that he will earn the next wage. By the fourth, Clatter stands sideways and begins browsing.

“You know this.”

Pell answers, “Yes. What are you practicing now?”

He can admit he wants to look prepared, ask for a different exercise, or end the session without discussing himself. The practical lesson holds across answers: repeat a useful signal, change the situation, then stop. Clatter already crossed the plank. It has no reason to share his anxiety about looking employable.

They establish a short descending call, paired with an open palm, for making room on a narrow path. Sound or gesture can work separately. Ordinary training and reliability remain intact; the scene does not add an affection tax, random command failure, or lost combat skills. A missed optional conversation cannot make the later partnership defective.

## A guest at the kitchen door

Before the paid departure, a short household visit makes the relationship more than a private bond. These use the proposed names in [Family at Home](Family%20at%20Home.md).

- **Ada** notices Clatter rubbing against a newly repaired harness. She relocates the rough brushwood stand and watches it choose that instead. “There. A customer who knows what it wants.” It later recognizes her work apron without becoming obedient to every request.
- **Lina** letters a sign: PLEASE DO NOT APPLAUD THE LIVESTOCK. Clatter pushes its forehead beneath the board to scratch. She adds an arrow. The joke gives them something to discuss besides her brother's departure.
- **Tobin** imitates the rasp perfectly, then discovers that the sound alone does not make Clatter follow him. He begins watching its feet. **Nella** sits nearby inventing a competition in which staying completely still counts as winning. Clatter wins while chewing.

On a return visit, one of them can know a useful preference the protagonist missed. Affection does not give him exclusive expertise. When Clatter stays with a familiar keeper during another expedition, those relationships continue.

## First practical success: a way back through weather

This scene can sit inside [First Paid Journey](First%20Paid%20Journey.md), on a marked maintenance spur of the Bell Road. A gust turns the next stretch pale with cloud. A loose board knocks against a post. Clatter refuses the crossing; the protagonist initially looks ahead for something alarming.

The player can inspect the footing and noise, seek the marked longer path, or ask Pell to check. On inspection, the post moves under pressure. He and Pell secure the existing support with the supplied maintenance fastening. Clatter crosses after the board stops shifting. Its refusal was useful evidence with an ordinary, testable cause.

Fog still hides the return bend. Clatter answers a familiar grazing call, waits for the hillside echo, and takes the correct turn. The protagonist checks Pell's marker and the ground before following. Animal knowledge and human records agree. The report now identifies both the loose support and a return marker needing better visibility.

At the exchange, Pell writes both contributions into the account. The protagonist receives ordinary credit for completed work. Clatter finds a rough crate corner and presents its neck to him. Their first victory is a safer road and a completed commission; the grander significance belongs to what the player remembers.

The longer path remains valid, with its own useful survey observation. Following Clatter without inspection is never the uniquely correct answer. This is field cooperation, not a new spell, compulsory creature gate, or bonus combat action.

## Three returns that change the relationship

| Movement | Concrete return | Emotional payoff |
|---|---|---|
| **II — a foothold away from home** | He proudly introduces his first rented room. Clatter prefers the permitted sheltered yard because the lintel crowds its horns. He moves his evening seat outside sometimes; safe accommodation also exists when he travels without it. | Their life together includes different preferences. Success is real even when it does not resemble his imagined companion beside the stove. |
| **III — the voice that cannot refuse** | During the [rope ordeal](Horror%20and%20Fourth%20Wall.md#an-ordeal-the-protagonist-carries-out), Clatter presses twice against his leg. His voice keeps reassuring everyone; his rope hand cannot give the usual gesture, and his attempted call breaks. A familiar ally recognizes the mismatch and helps transfer the rope. | A mundane practice lets somebody notice distress. Clatter wants room to pass; it has not diagnosed the accord. Human observation, alternate signals, and the surveyed exit complete the rescue. |
| **IV — another departure** | He checks the route with someone else, accepts help with equipment, and gives the familiar signal. Clatter steps aside, then occupies precisely the place needed for the last bag. | Growth leaves room for an exasperating animal and a shared task. The farewell or homecoming can suit any of the still-open tonal endings. |

After the ordeal, Clatter may hesitate near a scraping door associated with that passage. Calm space and another entrance allow the relationship to continue. No forced death, betrayal, supernatural omniscience, or compulsory cure quest is required to prove that the bond mattered.

## When the player chooses differently

A Thimbleback relationship would grow through shared sorting, displaced possessions, scent, and a reliable retrieval routine. Its first practical success could recover a dropped marker beneath a culvert. An established handoff gesture, noticed by an ally, could matter in the later ordeal. It should not suddenly hear Bellwether echoes or inherit Clatter's crowd response.

Another companion needs its own practices. If the player travels without the first partner, a human ally recognizes previously established rope signals and physical distress. Optional companionship enriches the scene without controlling access to its successful exits. Clatter can recur with another keeper if its earlier circumstances support that; the player does not receive an identical replacement individual.

These are authored relationship proposals, not an implemented behavior simulation. [Companionship and Catching](../03%20Game%20Design/Companionship%20and%20Catching.md) owns invitations and refuge rules; [Tactical Combat](../03%20Game%20Design/Tactical%20Combat.md) retains the five-creature maximum. Field scenes grant no sixth combat participant. Species abilities and training remain available independently of whether the player watches every affectionate scene.
