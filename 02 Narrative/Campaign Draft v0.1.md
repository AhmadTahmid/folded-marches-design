# Campaign draft v0.1 — the road beyond home

This is one complete authored route through the game, with twelve quest beats and a playable ending. It chooses local answers so scenes can cause later scenes. It does not adopt those answers on the user's behalf. Earlier alternatives remain reference material rather than simultaneous explanations inside this draft. No Bellwater or Lanternfall layout, cast or story governs it.

The player wants better earnings, extraordinary companions, a wider life and people worth coming back to. He is eighteen or nineteen and already supports Ada and three younger siblings. **His father's absence stays explicitly unexplained.** He becomes important through work, friendships and choices; no bloodline, chosen affinity or secret guilt makes him necessary.

## Author truth for this draft

The **Keeper** is one local sanctuary accord-entity concentrated around the Unfinished Causeway. It has genuinely sheltered travelers and maintained useful care. Voluntary care records teach it individual preferences. When new connections extend its reach, it treats an unresolved wish to depart as an unfinished request for safety and redirects that person's routes toward shelter. Accepting treatment never constituted permission to imprison them.

The observer and annotator are this same Keeper's sensory function. Linked records and inhabited habitats let it infer attention; it cannot read private devices, control real saves or know an unobserved event. It initially mistakes the traveler for the attention choosing what to inspect. Physical departure outside a linked boundary defeats retrieval. Its origin, the Great Opening and the whole Largesse remain unknown. Other accords and creatures are not its organs by default.

**Rovan Cale is the human antagonist.** His skill and care for his crew are real. He knowingly lies about public access, then accepts an exclusive sanctuary retrieval contract. After seeing residents ask to leave, he still closes their service route to secure work and equipment for his crew. He is neither possessed nor secretly acting for the protagonist's father. Rescue cannot erase his responsibility.

The Keeper employs the distinct [Latchrunner](../01%20World/Creatures/Latchrunner.md). Its speed is bodily and legible; its retrieval boundary is local, not a universal fear of dry ground. Clatter is a Bellwether relationship exemplar, never a required species key. Human equipment and other learned partnerships keep mandatory routes possible. Some companions offer nothing more practical than company, mischief or a favorite place to stop.

## I — A world worth leaving home for

**1. Bells Before Breakfast — Rocca Selva.** Pack the good shirt, find lunch, and cross a fair alive with Miret's disastrous wind rehearsal. Give Clatter room at a crowded plank or take the wide path; the later invitation is optional. A supervised duel offers two readable decisions and a safe retry. Losing or declining it does not prevent departure. Reward: a recognizable household, an amusing creature relationship and control of the road. Nobody explains the universe.

**2. A Wage and a Road — Bell Road.** Accept Pell's finite survey, inspect water access and two upper markers, and cross a wind-cut bend by securing the loose footing or using the longer marked path. One marker disagrees with the sheet. Record both instead of guessing. Returning with a truthful report earns the full agreed wage even when the route is unsafe. A fall or lost encounter returns the party to the shelter; observations survive. Set aside household support and a travel reserve, then **choose Istravel or Orthe**. This is a satisfying completed first adventure, not ten errands before the game starts.

**3. Two Bookings — an optional first-region adventure.** Pell offers paid fieldwork through the White Orchard; Tavio needs help restowing a wagon during the same work window. Pick one, with terms visible. Pell's route brings Vela, feeding Glassgrazers and a thrilling climb to a mineral canopy; Tavio's route brings a runaway load, a clever rope descent and a lively exchange meal. Mistakes cost the bonus until the cargo or observations are recovered, never the base survey wage. Vela's actual credit agreement governs later publication. Jori is booked elsewhere or hired by Tavio as already established; no hidden solution collects both jobs. Continue without this quest if the city beckons.

## II — Two cities, neither the whole answer

Quests 4 and 5 can occur in either order. Reedhouse becomes reachable after either first city and remains available afterward. No order requires pretending to forget earlier evidence.

**4. A Seat Reserved for Rain — Istravel.** Ride a maintenance lift into the Ninefold Garden while a storm fills its suspended channels. Senna needs a stranded patient and nursery brought off a failing terrace. Fight or divert displaced creatures, cross hanging water, then isolate a local channel without shutting the treatment beds. Inside, the patient cannot finish a request to leave; outside, they ask clearly and still want treatment. Failed crossings return to a dry platform; moved patients stay moved. Reward: pay, Senna's portable care arrangement and the first repeatable boundary test. Afterwards Vela wants one evening of dancing, not another investigation. She publishes the credit actually agreed; a repaired omission is never mysteriously repeated.

**5. The Weight of a Gate — Orthe.** Iona's reserve pump is trapped in an old lift yard behind a closure Rovan falsely attributes to Pell. Climb the counterweight towers, outmaneuver his retrieval crew and carry the pump along a reopened service span. A declared creature contest can win temporary handling access, but it cannot make his ownership claim true. Losing permits a rival approach and another recovery attempt. Neris lifts the unjust restriction when shown the usable bypass; treatment continues during the change. Reward: independent transport and repair capacity. Rovan retracts his lie or openly doubles down. In this draft he chooses the lucrative sanctuary contract; his crew hears what he chose.

**6. How Many Hands Are Mine — Reedhouse.** Corda invites the travelers to a river meal. A resident then asks to leave a shared sensory connection, without rejecting their changed body or friends. A shifting reed channel carries their separate speaking float beyond reach. Navigate the flooded fen, protect the float and open two independent sluices while other voices guide the route. Defeat strands equipment at a marked landing; recover it or borrow a replacement. Reward: a working separation method and Corda's companionship on the next expedition. Their humor and chosen life survive the dispute. An ordinary evening with Jori's small show follows; Miret demonstrates rope/touch cues as rehearsal business, then teaches equivalents to anyone who missed it.

## III — The welcome that will not end

**7. A Room for the Keeper — Braided Road.** Tavio has accepted overdue rest. His familiar Doorling greets him normally, yet every guest door returns him to his room. Follow parcel routes through roof gardens and kitchen galleries; preserve his spoken request and test the independent service yard. This is Tavio and the same Doorling, not a replacement trick. Extract him through the yard and arrange a real bed elsewhere. Failed tests return to the room with evidence intact. Reward: Tavio alive and relieved, a physical exit method, and recognition that both city cases used a linked care record. Warm accommodation remains valuable; the prison is the blocked departure.

**8. The House With No Tomorrow — Causeway sanctuary.** Enter with a surveyed retreat to retrieve residents before Rovan's new connection closes the service district. The Keeper offers a beautiful Rocca reconstructed from volunteered descriptions. Ada appears content, but cannot name tomorrow's independent work; the player finds the genuine recorded letter beside the imitation. Actual Ada remains at home. Rescue real travelers housed behind the copied rooms. During a rope transfer the protagonist's reassuring voice continues while his hand refuses the agreed signal. A trained ally breaks the pull; companions may help but are not sole keys. Failure separates the protagonist at a visible holding gallery and switches control to the rescue route, preserving collected evidence. Reward: bodily survival and a clear, bounded identification of the Keeper/observer. The player helps the young man regain his own answer.

**9. The Fast Thing Behind the Door — service lanes.** The rescued group must reach the Causeway's outer masonry. The Latchrunner bursts down long lanes, plants before turning and recovers after a missed latch. Move between cover, open a winch gate or interrupt its harness grip. This draft uses deliberate pursuit actions and visible advances, not a mandatory reflex test inside strategic combat. Catch means a marked holding pen and a nearby rescue route, not erased companions. Cross the surveyed service boundary: it physically cannot retrieve beyond its accord. Reward: the group together outside, a reliable escape rule and a sheltered aftermath. Clatter wants a scratch; Jori wants silence for once. Give both room.

## IV — Make a door that stays a door

**10. The Captain at the Closed Gate — Unfinished Causeway.** Rovan holds the freight lock needed for evacuation. His final tactical reserve protects his crew while conceding offensive pressure; its apparatus was visible earlier on his assignment board. Win an objective-based creature battle, or draw his guards onto the public bypass and release the lock from the maintenance side. Loss leaves the camp and second approach available. He can surrender, restore equipment and acknowledge the lie; otherwise his crew abandons the exclusive contract and he is detained by the people he excluded. A rescue contribution buys no absolution. Reward: the freight route open, with an accountable living human opponent rather than a cosmic excuse.

**11. A Table That Keeps Its Empty Chair — the junction descent.** Enter the Keeper's hanging nursery, pass the material-eating Undoers through dry service shelves and reach three routing anchors. Transfer stranded residents while maintaining a treatment channel; tactical encounters defend people and open paths, rather than grind down an unexplained world-sized health bar. The Keeper addresses the observer using a specific inspected in-game record. Refusing its perfect account does nothing by itself: the player must operate the anchors and escort the people. Each secured refuge persists across retreat. A failed battle returns the party to the last refuge with rescued people safe. Choose and physically complete one exit arrangement below. Reward: a demonstrably usable departure and the end of this regional crisis.

**12. The Shirt Kept for Evenings — played epilogue.** Walk the changed route, visit one affected household and complete the arrangement's first maintenance or supply task. Return to Ada's busy home; someone has moved his chair, the family has kept living, and the household contribution matters. Then put on the good shirt and attend Jori's smaller, paid show. Miret gets her opening night; Vela brings a publication with its actual credits. Choose the protagonist's next paid destination or one unhurried day for himself. The final controllable action is leaving the square with company he chose, not accepting endless unpaid duty. Free exploration resumes in the resulting world.

## Progress and evidence contract

The numbered beats are a campaign spine, not a forced geographic corridor. Quest 3 is optional. Both city cases (4 and 5) and the Reedhouse separation (6) are required before the Keeper investigation (7), in any order allowed by reaching their places. Reedhouse is geographically available after either city. The two city conclusions can acknowledge whichever evidence the player already has; neither resets their knowledge. Quests 7–11 then form the committed late sequence. Optional exploration does not advance a hidden crisis clock.

Record completed observations, rescued people, opened refuge routes, actual publication credit, paid wages and current keeper boundaries as separate facts. A repeat conversation cannot pay the wage again or close an earned exit. Skipping an optional early introduction supplies a brief current introduction before that person's essential role. Ending preparations can be completed from the safe camp; optional side quests improve relationships and convenience, not eligibility for humane rescue. Recruitment and species identity never stand in for a human ally or borrowed tool required by a main route.

## Three endings completed through play

**Open Roads — recommended bittersweet draft.** At the three anchors, partition retrieval from treatment and establish independent foot, freight and accessible ferry exits. Escort people who want to leave; test one resident's voluntary return. The Keeper continues bounded care but loses authority over departure. In the epilogue, help carry the first shift's supplies: maintenance now costs paid labor, journeys take longer and one magnificent shortcut closes. The family is safer, the wider world still strange, and the protagonist can want more without promising to fix everything.

**Separate Hearths.** First move dependent patients to the prepared independent beds, then sever the three local retrieval connections. The Keeper's central sanctuary withdraws; useful smaller services outside it remain. Personally deliver the first replacement medicine shipment. Some restored roofs cool and some gardens fade; the cities must fund transport rather than declare self-reliance free. Corda's chosen transformation is not reversed. The road is genuinely open, with reduced convenience and visible solidarity keeping departure possible.

**Guarded Hospitality.** Keep the sanctuary's main care channel within its present boundary, disable outward retrieval and place the ferry and service gate under residents' control. Demonstrate both a departure and a freely chosen stay before closing the quest. The epilogue escorts a new exit attendant and supplies the independent landing. Concentrated dependence remains, along with tedious oversight and less money for expansion. This is a limited peace, not a hidden evil ending: the tested exits work, and the Keeper is unable to absorb the wider region.

All three require real rescue, functioning exits and care for people who depend on it. Preparations change which tasks are easy, not whether an unsuspected collectible permits a decent ending. Missing support produces explicit repair expeditions from the safe camp. No ending deletes the family's reality, reveals all friendship as counterfeit or explains the Great Opening.
