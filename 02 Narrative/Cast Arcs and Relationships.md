# A cast that changes together

This draft develops the user's request for strong recurring characters through [four new traveling people](../01%20World/Traveling%20Cast.md), the existing [regional cast](../01%20World/People%20of%20the%20Marches.md), and [Family at Home](Family%20at%20Home.md). Specific scenes, relationships, and outcomes remain proposals. No compulsory death, romance, betrayal, or fixed final comfort level is selected.

The protagonist is already a provider at eighteen/nineteen. His peers widen his life: competition, enjoyable wasted evenings, disappointment he can survive. Affection alone need not settle a dispute about wages, credit, or safety.

## A recurring scene has three jobs

[Companion Quests and Camp Conversations](Companion%20Quests%20and%20Camp%20Conversations.md) expands these principles into four personal quest chains, three individual-creature studies and short between-job exchanges. Existing publication corrections, commission exclusivity and Rovan's accountability remain intact. These are scene developments, not new adopted biographies, fixed outcomes or compulsory romances/deaths. The user's request for a wider deep ensemble is answered by expanding meaningful returns before adding another cluster of introductions.

Each substantial return shows **recognition, independent change, and a new choice**. Vela remembers an argument; her printed sample exists; the protagonist considers another collaboration. Jori repeats a bad stage entrance, but now his show has a booking and helping means agreeing to a defined shift.

Use passing encounters, shared tasks, and aftermaths. Letters or reports from mutual acquaintances can establish independent action when someone misses a meeting; they cannot invent player choices.

These optional scenes sit inside [First Region](First%20Region.md). Give missed characters equivalent introductions before major demands. The sequence preserves [First Paid Journey](First%20Paid%20Journey.md)'s money, deadlines, and exclusive work choices.

## Relationships that do not run through the protagonist

| Relationship | Initial exchange | Concrete fracture | Possible repair or continuing difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vela and Pell** | Methods, illustrations, a professional reference | Her publication gives a cleaner impression than her evidence warrants | She includes a correction and Pell signs a narrower claim; both remain ambitious about different things |
| **Vela and Jori** | He stages a demonstration; she lends a painted backdrop | She treats preparation as casual help and gives unsolicited performance notes | A fee, a credit, and permission before giving notes; their next demonstration is funnier and clearer |
| **Jori and Tavio** | Reliable lifting in exchange for fair work | Jori misses a booking; Tavio realizes he taught him to overpromise by example | Repayment and fewer commitments; Tavio must also stop calling every request a small favor |
| **Jori and Miret** | Voice coaching and enthusiasm about a show | He improvises away a cue another performer needs; she humiliates him while correcting it | He restores the cue; she apologizes publicly. Rehearsal continues under a different agreement |
| **Rovan and Pell** | Mutual respect from difficult route work | Rovan falsely claims Pell approved excluding other carriers | He retracts the claim where he made it or loses their professional cooperation; a rescue does not erase the dispute |
| **Rovan and Vela** | Sponsor access to unusually good observation sites | Access requires delaying a shared route finding that other workers need | She rejects or discloses the restriction; accepting it preserves opportunity and damages specific trust |
| **Miret and Senna** | Effective care followed by an overdue musical invitation | Senna tries to fit the event into a public endorsement Miret never agreed to | They separate the performance from the endorsement; affection and professional boundaries survive |
| **Ada and Jori** | A properly quoted covering repair | Jori asks for another revision after the price was agreed | He pays for the extra work or simplifies his design; the protagonist need not negotiate on Ada's behalf |

These scenes can occur before the protagonist arrives or during an optional visit. Their outcomes follow the participants' authored choices, with player involvement where useful. He is neither universal mediator nor the only person able to apologize.

## Vela: closeness without compulsory agreement

Her main sequence is **competitive meeting → possible collaboration → agreed or disputed credit → a harder test**. Give them a scene with no lesson: dressing for a public dance while each pretends to know where to stand. Either can leave early, join other company, or watch an absurd local contest. Social pleasure need not imply flirtation.

Publication respects the White Orchard agreement. A corrected early attribution stays corrected; a sponsor's later request to remove it tests Vela under new pressure. If credit remained unresolved, show what was actually contributed and promised before confronting the omission. Separate accounts remain separate. Where no player contribution is owed, a later failure can concern Pell's analysis, with Pell pursuing their own remedy. Vela can correct the public record on her own initiative. Distance or a declined collaboration remains valid; never fabricate a breach merely to repeat an apology arc.

In Movement III she has obligations beyond accompanying him. She may contribute a map, equipment, or a day's help while declining the dangerous expedition. Caring about someone does not require joining every mission. Their final relationship can be intimate friendship, friendly professional rivalry, or respect at a distance; romance remains an author decision.

## Jori: the friend who asks for something

His sequence is **useful colleague → welcome company → unreliable promise → a smaller honest commitment**. The show gives him a plot with visible progress: a borrowed room, a bad rehearsal, a paid repair, posters, a performance. Progress advances at authored visits or agreed story transitions, not an invisible clock that punishes exploration.

His missed freight booking is his responsibility. The player may help negotiate a repayment schedule or simply listen; cannot erase what happened by delivering a better joke. Later, when the protagonist is recovering, Jori offers a defined evening of help and says which morning he must leave. Keeping that small promise matters more than declaring himself available forever.

Jori can tell the protagonist, affectionately, that every conversation has become about his next responsibility. Sometimes they need to attend the show. Helping may mean watching without fixing anything.

## Rovan: an opponent who acts

His sequence is **earned respect → attractive offer → visible exclusion → confrontation with a lasting result**. The route obstruction should interrupt actual work: another carrier cannot reach a paid collection; a public marker has been obscured; Pell's name is attached to a statement they did not make. Investigating these facts leads to Rovan, not a late confession delivering the whole plot.

Give him an initiative after being challenged. He can offer a narrower private deal, publicly defend the closure, or remove his own equipment and abandon the lucrative contract. His choice depends on the developed dispute, not an invisible friendship score. The protagonist can refuse to work with him and still need to coordinate a later rescue. Cooperation during danger does not silently restore access rights or settle compensation.

If Rovan becomes a sustained adversary, his crew is not one obedient personality. A worker can leave, another can defend the pay, and a third can insist on compensation while staying. No sudden massacre is needed to prove that he has crossed a line. His strongest opposition comes from knowing the same work as the protagonist and being willing to restrict other people's choices to secure his own.

## Miret: a small encounter with a later life

Her sequence is **passing amusement → mutual acquaintance → useful preparation → witnessed failure and repair**. At first she is busy with the opera. Later Jori makes a recommendation, or Pell names her former crew experience. The link is legible without implying that she has been secretly following the protagonist.

Before the [later bodily ordeal](Horror%20and%20Fourth%20Wall.md#an-ordeal-the-protagonist-carries-out), a preparation scene practices signals under ordinary noise. Miret herself misses one during distraction; the group stops, identifies why, and chooses a clearer pattern. The technique is learned, fallible, and transferable. Later it helps people compare comforting words against an independently agreed action.

Her rewritten program interview supplies a quieter conflict about speaking for someone: an uncertain written answer becomes flattering confidence without the speaker's agreement. The original survives, and she can admit and correct ordinary editorial misconduct. Later she asks before helping tell another person's story. Her opera still needs an ending, and she still thinks hers is funniest.

Revisits show consequences in lived scenes: a repaired room, another person holding the keys, an argument interrupted by lunch. Keep careers, creature attachments, and ordinary enjoyment alongside the serious disputes.

## Keep future payoffs fair

Player evidence includes corrected publications, a paid invoice, changed work assignments, blocked equipment, and a rehearsed signal. Beliefs such as “Rovan always looks after his own” or “Vela has never had to work” are contestable character judgments. The author proposals above contain no chosen lineage, explanation for the absent father, or secret universal conspiracy.

Track promises and disputes in ordinary narrative terms: who knows what, what was agreed, what was repaired, and what remains owed. Later scenes acknowledge both reconciliation and distance. No optional relationship supplies the only comprehensible route to the ending. These characters offer additional skill, attachment, friction, and pleasure; humans do not add combat activations beyond the [five-creature rules](../03%20Game%20Design/Tactical%20Combat.md).
