# The first five hours — a road worth earning

**Authored opening package for implementation and review.** The user's Rocca build exposed a writing and experience problem: beautiful scenery did not explain why to approach Tavio or what Pell wanted, and the home village lacked enough inhabitants, interiors, commerce, creatures and surprises to sustain curiosity. This package supplies the experience to build. The build's success is user-reported; this writing pass did not inspect or change it. New fiction and numbers remain authored draft, not blanket user adoption.

The protagonist wants a first properly paid field reference, enough money to create breathing room at home, and a reason to cross the ridge he has spent his life carrying other people's luggage toward. **Today the roads reopen. For once, he wants a place on a wagon rather than another load to carry to it.** His mother and siblings have their own work and pleasures; home is neither a tragedy countdown nor an obstacle to escape.

## Fixed continuity for this package

- Rocca Selva is the existing independent Apennine-inspired village on three limestone contours. Keep its nine named districts and the regional route directions. Add subplaces, inhabitants and playable detail rather than replace the town with another reference demo.
- Morning begins before accepting Pell's commission. Getting dressed and packing lunch are preparation for fair/work seeking, not proof the contract was already signed. The good shirt is a small ambition, not a tutorial prompt about ideology.
- Tavio brings the arriving trade wagon, attractive goods and news. The player approaches because a remarkable creature and a stuck arrival are visibly interesting, and Tavio knows who is hiring. Helping Clatter is optional; he introduces Pell even if the player declines. Pell can also be approached directly.
- Pell's written finite survey pays **36 coins total**: 12 advance, then 24 on report acceptance. At acceptance, 8 of the advance goes into the agreed home contribution and 4 into the travel purse. At settlement, 8 goes home and 16 into the purse. The protagonist begins with **8 personal coins**. These numbers are new package fixtures; the prototype's 18 coins has no authority.
- Ada reviews the actual terms at her temporary market worktable before acceptance; she is reachable at home through the same scene if the player skips the market. Her review is one short conversation, not a permission quest. No repeated home journey is required. Food and emergency lodging through the commission are covered; basic route tools and an optional practice partner can be borrowed free.
- The survey has **three observations**: public water access at Cistern Crown, the marker at the upper Bell Road bend, and the next marker at the wind-cut bend. Record physical conditions and each actual numeral. Delivery is at the **Bell Road exchange**, where Pell visibly goes ahead after acceptance. An unsafe route earns the same base pay. Repairing it, winning practice or recruiting a creature is not a wage condition.
- One marker's disagreement is a small factual discrepancy. No mandatory ominous sting, private-device trick or hidden father connection. Ordinary mechanical, animal and human causes remain real.
- After settlement, **Two Bookings** remains optional and exclusive: Pell's White Orchard demonstration pays 18 more coins (4 household / 14 purse); Tavio's wagon handling pays 10 (2 household / 8 purse). Both terms and outcomes are visible. Declining both is valid. A committed booking advances that work window only when the player confirms departure; menus and wandering have no hidden deadline.
- On Pell's branch, Tavio hires Jori. On Tavio's branch, Jori has his separate booking; he does not simultaneously help for free. Vela can be met on the public road regardless, with a substantial field scene on Pell's branch. Later introductions acknowledge missed meetings.
- All branches reconverge at an evening fair and household visit. The town's work has visibly advanced; there are new conversations and open doors. A city passage or another local day is a chosen next step. No five-hour compulsory lock confines the player to Rocca.
- Core combat remains the current individual-activation draft. Early encounters use one to three partners and available loans. Five is a later ceiling. Shared actions and alternative creature cards remain separate studies.

## Experience requirements for completion

The package includes a paced representative five-hour route, a shorter critical route and optional depth; actionable scene scripts and journal directions; connected exterior/interior geography with encounters placed; recurring residents with phase locations and conversations; shops and prices; invitations and owned/unavailable creature wonders; side stories with consequences; both booking branches; battle/field encounter rules; retreat, missed-content and return handling; and an implementation handoff whose identifiers can be checked across pages.

Authored durations remain estimates until the user's build is played. Writing completion means specific content and causal structure to implement and review the intended first five hours, not a guarantee that every player takes exactly that long. The audit distinguishes source coverage and continuity from untested enjoyment, duration and presentation.
