# Opening script v0.1

Companion to [Campaign Draft v0.1](Campaign%20Draft%20v0.1.md). The first twenty to thirty minutes are a pacing target, not a measured duration. Times expand with exploration. Each spoken line appears separately with its speaker; italic directions are staging notes, never a “Narration” box. Advance, skip and revisit remain available. Scenes grant no new combat rules or final actor design.

## 0–4 minutes — a good shirt

*Morning light crosses the kitchen. Three small lunch bundles sit beside the protagonist's larger bag. Ada shakes dust from his better shirt. The player chooses whether to pack it on top or beneath the work clothes; neither changes wages.*

**Ada:** “For surveying?”

**You:** “They might have evenings.”

**Ada:** “I hope they do.”

*She folds it carefully. Tobin is trying to draw a creature with six identical left feet.*

**Tobin:** “Bring me one of these.”

**You:** “A creature, or a better drawing?”

**Tobin:** “Both.”

**Ada:** “We've covered this week's food. Your survey money helps with the roof.”

Player reply: **“I'll keep enough for travel.”** / **“I want to see beyond the ridge.”**

**Ada:** “Good. Come back with something to tell us.”

*Take the bag and gain movement. The father is not mentioned or explained. Optional household inspection shows Ada's own unfinished paid work and the other two siblings' activities. None becomes a chore list.*

## 4–9 minutes — everybody helps too loudly

*The square opens onto mountains and moving cloud. Miret coaches three apprentices. Their imitation gale coincides with a real gust taking her page.*

**Miret:** “Excellent. The weather can take the solo.”

*The page lands nearby; returning it earns a joke, not a required quest chain. Beyond, a Bellwether stops on a short plank as people clap.*

**Tavio:** “It crossed before there was an audience.”

Player actions: ask spectators for room, open the wider gate, or leave. Either useful approach clears the pressure. Clatter moves, stops to rub its neck against an ugly post, then finishes crossing.

**You:** “You knew where you wanted to go.”

**Tavio:** “I was the one having trouble.”

*Away from the crowd, Clatter presses twice against the protagonist and steps back. Offer a scratch or give space. Tavio permits an invitation; the creature approaches voluntarily, returns briefly to his wagon, then chooses the path.*

**Tavio:** “You can travel together. You don't have to become its whole world.”

*Declining leaves Clatter with familiar care. Another partnership can develop later; practice has a loan partner.*

## 9–12 minutes — money before mystery

*Pell lays a route sheet over a crate. The two marker sketches are clearly different.*

**Pell:** “Check the cistern and the upper markers. Bring back what you find.”

**You:** “And the pay?”

**Pell:** “Written here. A damaged road pays the same as a good one.”

**You:** “I'm looking for longer work after this.”

**Pell:** “Then give me a report I can trust.”

*Accepting records the task and visible agreed payment. The camera briefly follows the road toward the overlook, then returns control. No forced city-history lecture.*

## 12–22 minutes — a small road, a real choice

*At the cistern, inspect the flow and leaf-clogged grate. Record it; the survey does not require crafting a repair. A signed practice corner offers a detour.*

**Mara:** “Want a short match? I can lend you a partner.”

**You:** “Does Pell need me to?”

**Mara:** “No. I thought you might enjoy it.”

*Accept or continue. On accepting, show the opponent's visible charge and legal commands. The first ordinary attack is survivable; interrupting changes the outcome visibly. Explain costs at the command, not in a speech. After defeat, restore the practice partners and offer retry or leave.*

**Mara, after defeat:** “Now you've seen that charge. Another go?”

**Mara, after victory:** “You made that one count.”

*Back on the road, wind shakes a loose plank. Inspect and secure the supplied fastening, ask for help, or walk the longer marked path. Clatter responds to firmer footing if present; no companion is required. The overlook rewards either approach with the valley view.*

**You, at the first marker:** “Fourteen. Easy to see coming back, too.”

**You, at the second:** “Fourteen again. The sheet says sixteen.”

Player observation: record both readings. No ominous sting. Nearby, Vela follows hoof marks away from the seeds she identified.

**Vela:** “I found your mysterious animal.”

*She holds up a seed-covered pack strap.*

**Vela:** “Very persuasive luggage.”

**You:** “Are you changing the drawing?”

**Vela:** “The caption. The drawing was excellent.”

*She corrects it. Invite further conversation or keep moving; the White Orchard can wait.*

## 22–30 minutes — paid, and not finished living

**You:** “Clear water. Leaves at the outlet. Both stones say fourteen.”

**Pell:** “Keep the two readings. I'll check the older list.”

**You:** “That's everything?”

**Pell:** “That's the job. Here's your wage.”

*Payment and survey completion appear once. Household contribution and travel reserve are explicit. Optional Jori encounter: a small creature knocks over the baskets receiving his rehearsal.*

**Jori:** “Difficult audience. Very little interest in language.”

*At home, the protagonist leaves the agreed contribution beside Ada's work.*

**Ada:** “Which road?”

Player: **Istravel's gardens**, **Orthe's high gates**, or **one more day here**. The choice opens travel; local optional work remains available.

**You:** “I've earned the first part. I'd like to see the rest.”

**Ada:** “Then keep the shirt.”

## Late scene — Rovan at the freight lock

*Workers carry stretchers behind the gate. Rovan's chalkboard assigns every crew member a return route. His safety reserve is visible before battle.*

**Rovan:** “Hear the whole offer.”

**You:** “They asked to leave.”

**Rovan:** “And next winter? Who carries them then?”

**You:** “We'll carry them today. You don't get to close the road until the future suits you.”

**Rovan:** “My crew finally owns what it works with.”

**You:** “Then tell them what you bought it with.”

*A crew member takes her name off the exclusive assignment. Player chooses the declared tactical confrontation or the surveyed bypass. After surrender, returning the gate equipment and acknowledging the false closure precede any offer of help. No apology automatically repairs trust.*

## Late scene — the Keeper's last welcome

*Residents wait beside a treatment channel. The final exit is physically closed. The Keeper speaks through the linked record; it cites a page the player really inspected, with a tested substitute if that optional page was missed.*

**Keeper:** “You returned to the house page. I kept the house.”

**You:** “You kept a description.”

**Keeper:** “The observer wants you safe.”

**You:** “Then listen to me. Open the road.”

**Keeper:** “There is weather beyond it.”

**You:** “I brought a coat.”

*The player still must operate the chosen exit arrangement and escort residents. The argument grants no victory by itself. At the outer landing, someone chooses to remain for treatment and someone leaves. Both actions complete normally. After the final household visit, the good shirt appears at Jori's show: survival leaves room for pleasure and another journey.*
