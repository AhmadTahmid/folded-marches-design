# The first paid journey

The protagonist wants better earnings for the family he already supports and a road of his own. [First Five Hours](First%20Five%20Hours.md) now owns this opening's chronology. [Opening Day Script](Opening%20Day%20Script.md) supplies the complete spoken scenes; [Two Bookings Script](Two%20Bookings%20Script.md) supplies both optional afternoon adventures. These replace the earlier sequence in which Mara first assigned three errands and Pell's survey was an additional posting after a trip home.

## A reason to go to the yard

Morning begins before he has signed anything. Tavio arrives with goods and work notices. The protagonist is interested in the job, the extraordinary working creatures and the prospect of going beyond the places where he normally delivers other people's loads. Helping the arriving wagon or meeting Clatter is optional; Tavio still points out Pell, and the notice allows a direct approach.

Pell offers a finite survey with three observations: public water access at Cistern Crown, the upper Bell Road marker and the wind-cut bend's marker/footing. Deliver the report to Pell at the Three-Awning Exchange, identifiable by its red-tile roof. Pell visibly goes ahead after acceptance; the vacated village station repeats these directions.

## Terms before acceptance

Ada checks the written terms at her market worktable, or at home if she has not moved yet. This is a short household conversation, not a permission errand or a mandatory return journey. Her harness work contributes to the roof fund too. The agreed first absence is covered, without a daily household hunger meter.

He begins with eight personal coins. Tools, commission food, safe rest and necessary emergency lodging are covered. An unsafe finding earns the same base pay as a clear route. Repairing the plank, buying gear, recruiting an animal or winning a battle is not required. These scoped draft numbers supersede the unrelated prototype's eighteen-coin fixture.

## A report that changes something

The cistern tap runs; a separate wash outlet has leaves. One marker is hard to see uphill. Both actual marker numerals are fourteen, while the sheet expects sixteen at the wind-cut bend. Record the evidence without inventing a cause. Asta changes the exchange's route board according to the actual footing report. Pell pays the balance once and signs the protagonist's first field reference, usable as an introduction at either city's field office.

The loose footpath has a supplied fastening and a maintained cart-contour alternative. Vela's mistaken animal identification and the public wildlife overlooks reward taking interest. Optional supervised practice supports a loaned partner and free recovery. The complete field actions and dialogue are in the script, rather than left for the implementer to infer.

## One afternoon, two bookings

The base job is complete before either offer. Pell's White Orchard expedition pays eighteen more, split four home/fourteen purse, with a canopy adventure and a field-office contact. Tavio's wagon handling pays ten, split two home/eight purse, with a freight descent and practical relationship. Pavo's injured ankle is cared for in every branch. If the protagonist chooses Pell or neither, Tavio hires Jori and buys less cloth. If the protagonist helps Tavio, Jori has his separate booking. No hidden solution performs both appointments or collects both wages.

Confirm departure to commit. Browsing offers and exploring have no hidden countdown. Retreat preserves booked progress; explicit withdrawal forfeits only the unfinished extension fee. Declining both leaves public roads and future work available. [Two Bookings Script](Two%20Bookings%20Script.md) owns exact objectives, recovery, Vela's credit and branch dialogue.

## Return with a story

The representative route returns to the evening fair and a household that kept living. The already allocated contribution is acknowledged, not charged again. The player chooses a true thing to tell the family, sees changes from local stories, enjoys Jori's rehearsal and plans Istravel, Orthe or another local day. Leaving earlier after the report is also supported; local stories have return variants.
