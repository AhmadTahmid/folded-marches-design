# First region — roads out of Rocca Selva

This chapter develops the approved next writing task: a first companion, a recurring fellow traveler, connected adventures, five creature kits, and quiet foreshadowing. The user additionally wants a strong recurring cast, including people who become important later. The events, new characters, precise sequence, and rewards below remain proposals.

The emotional movement is **from being the dependable boy at home to discovering what kind of traveler he can become**. He already supports his mother and three younger siblings. Better earnings matter; beauty, friendship, pride, and enjoyment matter too. Nineteen remains a working age, and his father's absence remains unexplained.

## What the region should give the player

By the time they choose a longer journey, the player should have a companion whose habits they recognize, a person they want to meet again, a practical success they earned, and a view they would visit without a quest marker. They should also have seen how another traveler approaches the same opportunity differently.

The chapter is a connected area of experience, not a compulsory completion list. [Bells Before Breakfast](Quests/Bells%20Before%20Breakfast.md) establishes home and a first departure. Either major city remains a valid next destination. A player can leave early, return to unfinished local adventures, or spend longer becoming familiar with the mountains.

## A hub and three outward loops

| Place / loop | What draws the player | Creature and practical discoveries | Human encounters |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rocca Selva hub** | Family, fair, paid work, companionship, gossip, ordinary creature games | First partnership, household creature care, safe practice | Ada and siblings; Mara and Tavio; one brief introduction to Miret |
| **Bell Road and White Orchard** | Paid survey, wind gardens, mineral branches holding tiny pools | Bellwether path behavior, Glassgrazer feeding routes, Brackenstride sightings | Pell's commission, Vela's fieldwork, Jori at the exchange; optional Rovan challenge |
| **Staired Wood and lower Old Switchback** | Gathering camp, music, sheltered stairs and the spring above the road | Thimbleback exchanges; Cragmantle signs and dependable tracking; alternative routes on foot | Jori's attempts at a performance; Vela may ask for a second opinion on a record |
| **Cloud Loom approach** | A useful bell delivery and colored veils above the ridge | Kitefin flights, weather signs, creature signals, a high shelter reached by several methods | A shared practical job with Jori; encounters with passing keepers |

These are named routes and sites from the [Atlas](../01%20World/Atlas.md), joined by ordinary return paths. The Cloud Loom approach is the accessible shoulder near keeper shelters; it does not grant immediate safe passage across the whole Skywash Traverse. Flight and an owned mount are unnecessary. The first region's exact size and travel times remain open.

## Opening sequence with room to wander

**Home before horizon.** Play a short household scene with competing requests, affection, and an agreed absence. Let the protagonist put something in his bag solely because he likes it. [Family at Home](Family%20at%20Home.md) owns those people. Mara's public duties remain distinct from his mother's concerns.

**Fair before exposition.** The player helps with the familiar opening errands and sees both cities' routes. Miret can be heard coaching three apprentices to imitate a gale across the square; nobody announces that she will matter later. New names should arrive through actions, not a roll call. The first close relationships remain the household, Mara, and Tavio.

**A partner with preferences.** [First Companion](First%20Companion.md) develops Clatter, one possible Bellwether partner from the fair. The player learns its request for space and its ordinary courage. A Thimbleback or another eventual approved starter has its own equivalent relationship; Clatter is not a compulsory key to the chapter.

**Work becomes a journey.** Pell's optional [First Paid Journey](First%20Paid%20Journey.md) offers clear pay and an honest reporting standard. The contract is discussed before the advance and packing. Bellwether refusal, a weather overlook, and a keeper exercise give the road texture. The choice between Pell's extension and helping Tavio remains meaningful; the new cast does not supply a hidden route to both rewards.

## Two adventures that make people memorable

[Under the White Branches](Quests/Under%20the%20White%20Branches.md) introduces **Vela Cotrin**, a young naturalist with paid equipment and a secure fallback. She is good at work the protagonist has barely encountered, but he can notice practical consequences her neat survey misses. The player can collaborate, challenge her attribution, or retain separate field accounts. She can become a friend without becoming an admirer who always agrees.

[A Bell Above the Clouds](Quests/A%20Bell%20Above%20the%20Clouds.md) gives **Jori Tern** a job and a desire at the same time: help equip a real shelter, then try a performance before an audience with nowhere urgent to go. Carrying and raising an ordinary bell turns into a small expedition with a view, a disagreement over doing too much alone, and a funny return. His stage ambition survives being useful to other people.

Neither quest is required for a later ending or contains a supernatural reveal. They establish behavior that later pressure can change. If skipped, Vela and Jori still have plausible subsequent introductions with their current work and appropriate greetings.

## A cast that can leave and come back

The detailed [Traveling Cast](../01%20World/Traveling%20Cast.md) and [Cast Arcs and Relationships](Cast%20Arcs%20and%20Relationships.md) own character history and recurrence. This chapter stages their first impressions:

- **Vela:** encounter a useful skill before the financial difference becomes uncomfortable. She can lend a tool casually; he can decide whether that feels welcome, awkward, or simply practical. Her later publication must acknowledge the work actually done.
- **Jori:** meet him as someone another person knows. If the player takes Pell's extension, he is the paid local help Tavio hires. If the player helps Tavio, Jori is met separately at the exchange or through the bell job. He does not work for free to erase the player's choice.
- **Rovan Cale:** an optional keeper exercise shows a captain who knows when to protect, when to pressure, and when to stop. A loss teaches something; it does not prove that the protagonist is worthless. Rovan can offer work the young man has good reasons to want. The terms create later conflict, rather than a sinister entrance announcing an inevitable betrayal.
- **Miret Sool:** the apprentices’ gale lesson is enough for now. A later meeting can recall a breathing exercise or her impatience with people who confuse loudness and clarity. Her future relevance comes from learned group-signal work, not concealed knowledge of the protagonist's origin.

Only one newcomer should demand sustained attention in a given scene. Existing people return between introductions. At a shared meal, let Vela ask Jori about his act while Pell repairs a map and the protagonist chooses whether to join the joke. Not every line is addressed to the player.

## Creature growth across the region

The early player normally travels with one to three battle partners. Recruitment is gradual and can be delayed; five is the maximum. The [First Region Creature Kits](../03%20Game%20Design/First%20Region%20Creature%20Kits.md) use five familiar species as a later regional design benchmark, not a compulsory collection checklist or a claim that all four techniques are immediately unlocked.

First teach a shared objective and protection, then a setup/payoff pair, then an opponent that removes protection or disrupts a sequence. [Worked Battle](../03%20Game%20Design/Worked%20Battle.md) illustrates coordination. [Counterplay Battle](../03%20Game%20Design/Counterplay%20Battle.md) asks what happens when the opponent answers it. Rovan may host a later challenge using that design, but the benchmark roster is not automatically his canonical lifelong team.

Human travelers converse, guide, and work. They add no sixth fighter or free combat activation. A guest keeper's creature, if ever selectable in an encounter, occupies one of the existing five places and follows its rules. Ownership, human friendship, and the active creature roster remain separate.

## One small uncertainty to take away

Use the already-written parcel discrepancy from the fair: the sender remembers six parcels, the tally records seven, and the sour flour has a real ordinary destination. Preserve the original note. A combined consignment or a broad substitution rule can explain it; no voice demands belief in a conspiracy.

The passing bell sound can remain an incidental acoustic uncertainty in a busy place. This pass adds no escalating sequence of impossible messages. Vela's observation and Jori's bell both work as learned; Clatter is not a lie detector. The player leaves with a question, not proof that their happy day was fraudulent. Later [mysteries](Mysteries%20and%20Revelations.md) can give the retained parcel account a second context.

## Closing the first circuit

At an authored return or carrier stop, the agreed household payment reaches home. A reply contains news he did not cause: his mother finished a job, a sibling argued with a friend, someone has moved his chair. An optional gift or drawing rewards attention; a missed purchase does not erase love.

Vela has her next destination. Jori has another booking. Rovan has positions to fill. The protagonist can accept an invitation, choose his own route, or remain near home for another expedition. The chapter ends emotionally when he can describe both what the journey gave his family and something he wants to pursue himself.

For the next writing pass, test one short scene twice: a warm reunion and a reunion after a broken promise. Each person should remember an actual event, retain a desire, and still be recognizable. These pages establish draft coherence; play duration, difficulty, and emotional effect remain untested.
