## Suggested routes, not a mandatory sequence

The routes below are wider exploratory alternatives. [Campaign v0.1](Campaign%20Draft%20v0.1.md) owns the active draft's eventual city/Reedhouse thresholds; this directory does not silently remove them or add an opening license requirement.

**September scene studies:** [Companion Quests and Camp Conversations](Companion%20Quests%20and%20Camp%20Conversations.md) develops four personal quest chains and three creature studies; [The Observer and the Sanctuary](The%20Observer%20and%20the%20Sanctuary.md) develops three central scene alternatives and a warehouse rescue; [Atmospheric Frontiers](../01%20World/Atmospheric%20Frontiers.md) provides local adventure briefs. They are not counted as additional dedicated, fully branched quest pages in the ten-quest directory above. Selected studies can later receive their own stable IDs, exact prerequisites, state branches and re-entry behavior.

**City-first adventure:** opening → either city → enjoyable exploration and creature encounters → other city or Reedhouse → homecoming → preparation and assembly.

**Wilderness-first adventure:** opening → White Road Moving → either city → Reedhouse or rival city → homecoming as desired → assembly.

**Transformation-first adventure:** opening → Braided Road → Reedhouse → one city → a second community's testimony → assembly, with the unvisited city represented through an invitation or later visit.

The common threshold is understanding a discrepancy, an affected person's preference, and a practical dependency. Completion of every optional quest is not a secret requirement for a comprehensible or worthwhile ending.

## What carries between quests

Carry concrete facts: a person agreed to testify; a clinic can provide traveling care; a road has seasonal passage; a recipe was retained; a delegate declined participation. Do not reduce these to a single reputation or moral alignment number.

If evidence changes, preserve the earlier account and let the protagonist amend an interpretation. If a relationship is damaged, give the person a recognizable reason and an appropriate repair opportunity. A quest marked complete can still have follow-up responsibilities without becoming an endless emergency.

## Quest writing checklist

For new entries, specify the attractive surface activity before the secret. Identify who wants what, who carries the labor, what can be learned without a particular companion, and what happens if the protagonist leaves. Include at least one choice that changes a relationship or practical condition. Make discovery order alter the question being asked, not just a line of greeting.

Not every future quest needs horror or an ideological argument. A lost performance troupe, a creature grooming contest, a ridiculous construction commission, a disputed racing shortcut, and an expedition to hear a rare weather sound could provide joy and character texture without concealing a cruel revelation. These are seeds for later discussion rather than completed quests.

## Current boundaries and open work

The ten pages develop a regional storyline, first-region character adventures, and connected side stories. Full dialogue, individual identities for several supporting applicants and visitors, exact reward balance, combat encounters, cinematics, schedules, and production scope remain to be developed after direction is reviewed. Narrative gates described here are design intentions, not implemented flags or tested quest logic.

[A Room for the Keeper](Quests/A%20Room%20for%20the%20Keeper.md) translates selected storytelling principles from the supplied Evertale commentary transcript (working-archive reference) into this draft's own cast and ecology. It is a proposal, not newly accepted canon; the transcript's inferred simulation story is not the explanation of this setting.

The cosmic origin, final comfort level, and protagonist model remain intentionally unselected.
