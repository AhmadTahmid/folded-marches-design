# Wiki v0.1 completion checklist

The milestone is **one coherent first design edition**, not every line and asset required to ship. A document pass can establish coverage and consistency; it cannot prove fun without play.

| Deliverable | Edition coverage | What remains later |
|---|---|---|
| Game identity and priorities | [Design overview](../00%20Foundation/Game%20Design%20v0.1.md) and [status](../00%20Foundation/Design%20Status%20v0.1.md) | User critique of the recommended direction |
| World and routes | Ten connected zones, optional warm forest, watersheds, access and return paths in [Regional Map](../01%20World/Regional%20Map%20v0.1.md) | Detailed layouts for each city/frontier; exact terrain scale |
| Independent home | Nine connected Rocca spaces, daily life, two loops, first walk and short local quests | Full room plans, final environmental art |
| Story through its ending | [Campaign](../02%20Narrative/Campaign%20Draft%20v0.1.md): four movements, twelve beats, local author truth, human antagonist and three played endings | Full later quest scripts and all order variants |
| Opening dialogue | [Opening script](../02%20Narrative/Opening%20Script%20v0.1.md), action choices, defeat/decline continuation and late scene samples | Timing in play, portrait/action staging |
| People and relationships | Existing household, recurring cast, personal arcs and camp scenes linked into the campaign | Complete reunion/relationship state tables |
| Creature discovery | [Roster](../01%20World/Creature%20Roster%20v0.1.md): nineteen encounter entries, eight detailed recruitment cards, linked full species lore | Numerical kits for later partners, final art; not all entries are collectible |
| Adventure systems | [Adventure rules](../03%20Game%20Design/Adventure%20Rules%20v0.1.md): movement, map, recruitment, Duet, growth, purchases, recovery | Balance, full economy and content databases |
| Tactical grammar | Existing timing/status rules, five numerical benchmark kits and worked battles; [combat review](../03%20Game%20Design/Combat%20Design%20Review.md) adds five identity briefs, five learning encounters and a three-round action-allocation comparison | Human review of comprehension and appeal, broader encounter roster, numerical balance and choice among alternatives |
| Delegation and sharing | [Build from the Wiki](Build%20from%20the%20Wiki.md), public reading order and versioned source | A later explicitly commissioned implementation task |

## After this edition

First review whether the places, creatures and story actually make the user want the game. Revise concrete weak points in this edition. Then author the second-city and late-rescue state variations, remaining recruitable kits and representative side-quest scripts. These deepen the existing game; they need not restart its premise.

When implementation resumes, use one bounded assignment with actual source files where integration is expected. Progress is a better scene, creature or system in this design, rather than the number of unrelated HTML demonstrations. Preserve useful animation/rendering lessons from all experiments.

No percentage of a finished commercial game follows from this table. The honest stage is: **campaign design and world structure drafted; opening scripted; content depth uneven; gameplay quality and final presentation awaiting later validation.**
