## Review performed on the source

Separate narrative, creature/economy and spatial reviews read the package against its existing owners. The coordinator checked the main dialogue and both branches, the representative route, state/economy ledger and handoff. This was an editorial and paper-state review, not simulated or human play.

- **Causal opening:** the protagonist seeks work before signing; Tavio's visible arrival and work notices motivate approach; Pell can be met directly. Ada reviews actual terms, and the report destination is always the exchange after Pell goes ahead. The changed route board gives the report a visible use.
- **Physical continuity:** schoolroom table and market occupy adjacent same-level spaces, so their carry uses the service ramp. Practice is at Pass House, separate from protected water. Strap Shelter and both road markers have an explicit order; the optional cache spur cannot block the wage route. Wagon loads remain tool barrel, jar crate and cloth bale through recovery and delivery.
- **Evidence and presence:** unexamined leaves and reverse-marker visibility are not narrated as known. Vela's missed introduction has a fallback. Creature individuals do not teleport from refuges. Owned visitors remain owned. Pavo's injury is established before the optional handling vacancy; later lines distinguish completion and withdrawal.
- **Story availability:** S04 remains available on the representative evening return. Ada's table/schoolroom arrangement follows S01; Lina's journal direction follows her actual location. The performance has explicit props, cues, alternative staging and an assistant fallback. The family shares only actual observed events.
- **Economy:** start 8 personal; base survey contributes 16 household and 20 personal, giving 28 personal before purchases. Orchard adds 4 household/14 personal, wagon 2/8; the mutually exclusive totals are 42 or 36 personal. Three paid local stories add at most 12. The sample 22-coin basket leaves 6 on the base-only route. Tools, necessary rest, observation access and a walkable onward route need no purchase.
- **Consequences:** a safe retreat retains progress; explicit booking withdrawal forfeits only its unfinished reward/contact. The unchosen work proceeds with other people. Later White Branches is a different study, acknowledges earlier credit and cannot pay the opening fee again. Invitations remain voluntary and distinct from side-story completion.
- **Combat:** the first three opening fixtures reference existing declared practice encounters; the wild cache defender has its own declared body, intentions, choices and recovery. Its worked damage uses existing cards, Armor/Ward and alternating activations. Neither the shared-action experiment nor alternate identity cards silently replace those rules.

## What is complete, and what evidence is still absent

The writing assignment has a connected source package with specific scenes, places, dialogue, interactions, alternatives and outcomes for implementation. No new runtime, art, animation or sound was produced by this pass. Final terrain fit, actual service/navigation behavior, save persistence and battle balance must be checked in the user's build.

The representative five-hour route includes optional local depth. A direct player may leave much sooner. The authoring estimate is not evidence of five measured hours, and a long script is not evidence of an enjoyable game. A later review should observe immediate comprehension, chosen detours, remembered people/creatures, a meaningful return and desire to see the next city. If those fail, revise the concrete weak scenes rather than adding travel time or compulsory chores.

Wider campaign detail is still uneven beyond this opening. That later writing remains a separate expansion; it is not hidden inside the claim that the first-five-hours source is ready to build.
