# Creature roster v0.1 — creatures worth meeting

**Draft design, not adopted canon or an implemented roster.** The [Bestiary](Bestiary.md) owns the eighteen existing species' anatomy and ecology. This page develops their playable encounters and adds [Latchrunner](Creatures/Latchrunner.md). Proposed colours preserve both expressive/bold and grand/elegant treatments under D-046. The borrowed Bellwater trail creature is not a canonical species or appearance.

## The whole field guide at a glance

Presence describes introduction, not rarity odds. Conditions follow [recruitment design](../03%20Game%20Design/Recruitment%20Eggs%20and%20Binding%20Instruments.md): understandable actions with retries. “Later partner” still needs an encounter and kit.

## Eight early and middle partnerships

Some encounters are quick meetings; others involve shared work and a later friendship scene. Kindness need not make every wild creature join.

### Thimbleback — the gift that keeps coming back

**Look and manner.** Preserve the knee-high shell-cup body. Chestnut plates, moss-green seams and a small apricot nose accent offer a warm, readable face. It waddles under a ridiculous load, then becomes brisk when anyone touches its collection.

**Meet and befriend.** Follow floating husks to a winter cache in a leaking drain. Open the side channel or carry the cache above water. On return, this individual offers a chipped blue tile, retrieves it when left behind, and offers it again. Accepting opens an invitation; declining leaves a friendly neighbor and repaired terrace.

**Play.** Cupguard and Clod Flick make a stubborn disruptor, not a team healer. Reach pressure or stripping protection answers it. Scent can reveal an optional lost keepsake also findable through tracks. At camp it stores the blue tile beside the player's boots. There is no sinister explanation for the gift.

### Bellwether — Clatter learns the road

**Look and manner.** Retain the compact grazer, gripping feet, ruff and crescent horns. A deep teal coat and warm ivory horns are an optional treatment, not approval of existing renders. Clatter's pale eye crescent, rasping first note and sideways shoulder presses make the individual recognizable in either art direction.

**Meet and befriend.** [Clatter's quest](../02%20Narrative/First%20Companion.md) owns the crossing: reduce the crowd, investigate the bell or take the wider path. A later walk and keeper agreement establish the invitation. Its next friendship scene is choosing an unfashionably rough scratching post for the family doorstep.

**Play.** Arresting Peal rewards spotting an unspent threatening action; Resolve and Steadfast provide readable counters. Ordinary horn-route recognition offers optional reassurance, with visual signs for everyone else. Later hesitation can motivate investigation, but Clatter is neither a lie detector nor the Keeper's secret voice.

### Embermole — a room that is allowed to be useless

**Look and manner.** Keep the stout six-clawed digger, copper whiskers and opening back plates. Coal-dark fur against a coral-to-amber glow should feel like warmth under a blanket. Before releasing heat it lifts the plates in sequence; when pleased, only the smallest plate flutters.

**Meet and befriend.** Help the baker open a cool nook for a crowded colony and light the backup stove. An independent adult explores the empty travel refuge during supper. Allow it to settle, offer a warm brick, or leave the refuge open for another visit. The invitation leaves dependent parents with their colony.

**Play.** Furnace Breath supplies Reach pressure; Ward, Silence and direct attacks threaten its plan. Coal Cast preserves a costly physical alternative. It can warm a camp seat, while blankets or a stove serve the same practical need. Its greatest camp triumph is falling asleep before anyone finishes thanking it.

### Kitefin — the fair's worst kite

**Look and manner.** Preserve the airborne ray and two streamers. A persimmon body inside pale opalescent fins gives a vivid centre without making the entire outline luminous. Excitement curls both tails; embarrassment involves a painfully elaborate second landing.

**Meet and befriend.** Follow a cloth thief to a blocked roost vent. Move a loose screen with the stallholder or open another air gap. The young adult returns the cloth badly folded. Offer it as a perch; one successful approach opens invitation. Later, choose a travel pennant from three ordinary scraps, with no correct colour.

**Play.** Proposed Crosswind Screen gives one ally a modest Barrier; a light Reach attack provides an alternative. Direct Reach pressure defeats careless exposure. It may carry a ribbon over a small gap; a thrown line works too. Weather associations stay observational, never prophetic.

### Doorling — the button nobody owns

**Look and manner.** The flexible keratin folds remain angular in motion and round at rest. Mulberry outer folds, a cream underside and bright copper eye-rims are a candidate palette. It hesitates with one foot lifted, then commits too confidently.

**Meet and befriend.** An unattached individual trades a tenant's brass button for three useless hinges. Follow its piles, return identifiable possessions and supply unclaimed scraps people agree it can keep. It leaves a hinge beside the travel refuge, waits, then steps inside when invited. Tavio's companion remains his own individual.

**Play.** Pick the Latch and Unknot overlap other partners' protection answers without supplying Strong Cleanse. Low health and Reach attacks punish poor placement. It can retrieve a visible dropped item from a narrow gap, also accessible with a hook. Its comic sorting persists through the story; one local nesting accord's confinement is not species-wide malice.

### Glassgrazer — the scratch-stone escort

**Look and manner.** Preserve six legs, long soft face and layered frosted fans. Dusty rose skin and pale celadon deposits suggest living warmth inside grandeur. It tests a rubbing stone with its cheek before the whole body follows.

**Meet and befriend.** Arrange a temporary opening onto a blocked mineral route, then accompany an adult past noisy carts. At the scratch-stone it rubs one fan, waits, and returns for the second side. A suitable refuge and keeper explanation open invitation; rejoining the herd also completes the encounter. Later, it rejects a polished substitute stone for the lumpy original.

**Play.** Shed Screen and Settling Pulse make it a durable rescue option. Silence, expended Focus and attacks on another ally limit that rescue. Shed minerals offer optional craft or keepsake material; shops provide alternatives. Beauty and useful clean water remain real even when particular institutions abuse accords.

### Brackenstride — a race with an unwanted shortcut

**Look and manner.** Keep the chest-high, four-legged russet runner and fernlike shoulder fans. Yellow-green fans over a dark plum throat can support exuberance or elegance. Braking spreads the fans before the feet gather beneath the body.

**Meet and befriend.** A courier's shortcut crosses a thorn nursery. Mark the detour, then follow an adult's playful challenge. Knowing the route and stopping at its final fan display completes the meeting; winning a twitch race is unnecessary. Invitations repeat. At camp it challenges someone carrying soup, receives a refusal, and races a leaf instead.

**Play.** Proposed Thorn Feint applies Exposed through a physical feint, overlapping Bellwether's opening role while remaining vulnerable to Disarm. Armorless opponents deny its payoff; confinement limits its modest offense. Optional riding rewards open routes, while walking or courier transport remains available. Speed does not make it a damage upgrade over early friends.

### Cragmantle — the climb with three pauses

**Look and manner.** Preserve the broad six-footed body, slate mantle, ocher belly and rosette pads. A muted blue slate against saturated warm undersides creates interest when it lifts a foot. It taps twice, pauses, and abandons unsuitable stone with majestic irritation.

**Meet and befriend.** Observe it reject a dusty hold. Brush the stone, inspect the next and follow using a borrowed safety line. At each pause, observe, rest or take the guide's ordinary route. A calm ascent opens invitation. A later visit shows it accepting company on its boulder, provided nobody takes the warmest patch.

**Play.** Proposed Settle In grants self Steadfast; deliberate Impact offense competes for the same activation. Strip and Reach pressure answer it. Climbing offers optional overlooks and an intimate pace, with guide-and-rope access to important destinations. Its patience should occasionally be funny, not a compulsory waiting chore.

## Team growth and role overlap

Begin with **one active friend**, or a keeper-lent practice partner while recruitment is undecided. Introduce a second after one understandable encounter, then widen choices through exploration. Five friendly creatures active is the eventual ceiling, not an opening instruction or a requirement to collect these eight in order.

Thimbleback, Glassgrazer and Cragmantle can anchor a team differently. Bellwether and Brackenstride create openings. Doorling, Kitefin, Embermole and Glassgrazer offer overlapping protection; Embermole and Kitefin reach past a front line. No species is the mandatory key to a story gate. A useful party can lack a particular cleanse and answer pressure through prevention, action order or retreat.

The five numerical kits remain in [First Region Creature Kits](../03%20Game%20Design/First%20Region%20Creature%20Kits.md); the other three have proposed signatures awaiting balance. Existing damage, Focus and status ownership stays there. No elemental chart or new turn economy follows. Recruitment need not reveal every technique in the first menu.

**Provenance and story boundary:** this pass develops the existing bestiary, Clatter, [companion scenes](../02%20Narrative/Companion%20Quests%20and%20Camp%20Conversations.md) and the user's preference for conditional recruitment and ordinary joy. The v0.1 Keeper is a local sanctuary accord entity acting as observer/annotator; it is not the origin of the Largesse. Latchrunner serves that local story. Most creatures remain animals with lives that do not secretly revolve around the protagonist.
