# Glossary

Working vocabulary for consistency. Names are proposals, not a claim that these are the only suitable terms.

| Term | Meaning | Authoritative entry |
|---|---|---|
| Great Opening | The transformative collapse and appearance of new ecologies roughly 160 years before the story | [History](../01%20World/History%20and%20the%20Opening.md) |
| Largesse | Public umbrella name for the new abundance and its underlying phenomena; origin and intention unresolved | [Largesse and Accords](../01%20World/Largesse%20and%20Accords.md) |
| Accord | A stable relationship among living beings, place, and an exception to familiar possibility | [Largesse and Accords](../01%20World/Largesse%20and%20Accords.md) |
| Fellow creatures | Provisional everyday label for the new beasts; not a final franchise name | [World Overview](../01%20World/World%20Overview.md) |
| Field accord / resting token | Proposed portable refuge used in companionship; details and name undecided | [Companionship and Catching](../03%20Game%20Design/Companionship%20and%20Catching.md) |
| Folded Marches | Working region name | [Atlas](../01%20World/Atlas.md) |
| Rocca Selva | Protagonist's Apennine-inspired mountain village | [Rocca Selva](../01%20World/Rocca%20Selva.md) |
| Istravel | City-state favoring accelerated cultivation of the Largesse | [Istravel](../01%20World/Istravel.md) |
| Orthe | Rival city-state favoring limits, reversibility, and civic independence | [Orthe](../01%20World/Orthe.md) |
| Confluence | Dispersed communities and schools exploring merged forms of life | [The Confluence](../01%20World/The%20Confluence.md) |
| Canon | Material the user has explicitly adopted, distinct from coherent working drafts | Wiki Guide (working-archive reference) |
| Player knowledge | Evidence encountered by the player; distinct from world events and NPC beliefs | [Quest and World State](../03%20Game%20Design/Quest%20and%20World%20State.md) |

See the world and narrative catalogs for names introduced in individual entries.
