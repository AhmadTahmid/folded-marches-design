# The Folded Marches — design wiki v0.1

A fantastical creature-companion RPG about leaving home, finding a wider life, and confronting a sanctuary that mistakes care for the right to keep you.

**Start with [Game Design v0.1](00%20Foundation/Game%20Design%20v0.1.md).** It is the reading spine for one coherent campaign draft. [Design Status](00%20Foundation/Design%20Status%20v0.1.md) distinguishes established direction from new author choices.

**Latest expansion: [First Five Hours — a road worth earning](02%20Narrative/First%20Five%20Hours.md).** The opening now has a fully scripted paid commission, a populated road-opening fair, enterable houses, shops and purchases, six local stories, creature meetings, two alternative afternoon adventures and a changed evening return. Its [handoff](04%20Production/First%20Five%20Hours%20Handoff.md) supplies the connected build packet. This opening replaces the older compressed scene order; the wider campaign remains in place. Playtime and enjoyment await the user's implementation and review.

This is an authored design draft with full story spoilers. It is not a finished game, accepted final canon or a promise of measured production capacity. Topic pages retain useful earlier alternatives; explicit v0.1 selections define this edition's working interpretation. Read the overview before combining them.

The design stands independently of Bellwater, Lanternfall and other production references. Their assets and source are not included in the public edition. Future implementations should describe this game's locations, creatures and events rather than relabel another demo.

The `v0.1` tag preserves the original campaign edition. Read `main` for the expanded opening and subsequent combat studies; the old v0.1 upload ZIP does not contain these additions.
